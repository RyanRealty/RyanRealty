## 0. The frame shift (the whole strategy in one line)

**Ryan Realty is a media operation that happens to sell real estate — not a real-estate operation that happens to post.** Every piece answers one specific question a real buyer or seller is asking right now. The video is information that comes FROM Ryan Realty, not an ad for a listing.

**Self-test before producing anything:** "Would a real person save this or send it to someone?" If no, rework the concept before spending a minute on production.

---

## 1. The three highest-leverage format bets (the default organic rotation)

1. **Hyperlocal neighborhood series (YouTube long-form → Shorts).** One 8-10 min "Living in [Neighborhood], Bend OR" per week, cut into a 45s Short. YouTube is a search engine — a good guide ranks for 2-3 years. Highest ROI per hour of production; a single hyperlocal channel has driven six-figure GCI.
2. **Monthly market-data drop.** ONE verified Supabase stat with a contrast hook — not a recap. "Bend's days on market just hit 38. Two years ago it was 11. Here's what that means for sellers this fall." 45-60s, Victoria VO. Saves + shares are the top-weighted 2026 signal and this format earns them.
3. **"What $X buys in Bend" (3-tier).** Three price points, three real properties, honest trade-offs. The hook reveals something buyers don't expect. Fastest viewer→DM converter because it answers exactly what buyers now search short-form for before Google.

**Dead / stop making:** the generic listing tour (walk-through + feature list + music). Saturated and algorithmically invisible on every platform. If a listing must be shown, frame it as one of the three formats above (e.g. "what $750K buys").

## 2. Hook + hold rules (2026, all platforms)

- **0.4s:** motion engaged (never static at frame 0).
- **1.0s:** on-screen text present.
- **First spoken word is content** — no "hey/today/welcome/let's talk about."
- **Hook carries a specific element:** a number, a place name, a contradiction, or a visual surprise.
- **First frame works as a thumbnail** (real photo + readable headline; never black / logo-only / blank). Ship-blocker.
- **Completion gate:** TikTok ~70% completion is the target; 5-second "qualified view" is the algorithm's trigger. Reels reward rewatch + sends.
- **Saves and DM-shares are weighted 3-5× likes** on IG + TikTok — design for "I need to save/send this."

## 3. Per-platform cheat sheet

| Platform | Length | Format that wins now | Cadence | Algorithm rewards |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **IG Reels** | 20-45s | Market-data drop, "what $X buys", carousel (save-engine) | 4-7×/wk | saves, sends, rewatch |
| **TikTok** | 21-45s | POV, myth-bust, hyperlocal hidden-gems, "what $X buys" | 1×/day if sustainable | completion %, comments, shares |
| **YouTube Shorts** | 30-60s | Cut-downs of the long-form neighborhood guide | 3-5×/wk | viewed-vs-swiped, sub conversion |
| **YouTube long-form** | 8-12 min | Neighborhood guide, market deep-dive, relocation guide | 1×/wk | watch-time, search ranking (durable) |
| **Facebook Reels** | 20-45s | Same as IG (cross-post), skews older/seller | 4-7×/wk | shares to groups |
| **LinkedIn** | doc carousel + native text | Market-insight carousel, sold-deal as market signal | 2-3×/wk | dwell, comments |

Cross-post but don't blind-dump: strip the TikTok watermark, re-caption per platform, lead with the platform's preferred hook.

## 4. Hook library (copy-ready, swap the specifics)

- "This $750K Bend home has a [secret/problem/feature] nobody mentions…"
- "POV: you just moved to [neighborhood] and discovered…"
- "Nobody's talking about what happened to [neighborhood] prices."
- "Bend's [stat] just hit [number]. Here's what that means for [buyers/sellers]."
- "What $[X] actually buys you in Bend right now."
- "3 things I'd tell anyone buying in [town] this year."
- "The most underrated neighborhood in Bend is…"
- "Sellers in [area]: this one number changes your timing."
- "I tour homes for a living. This is the [best/weirdest] thing I saw this week."
- "Everyone moving to Bend asks me the same question…"
- "[Town] vs [Town]: where your money goes further."
- "The Bend market in 30 seconds — no fluff."

Every hook must be backed by a real, §0-verified fact. Never a fabricated number for a better hook.

## 5. AI-native formats + the slop line

AI lets us make things that earn "how'd they make that": impossible camera moves, earth-zoom-to-property, seasonal transforms of a real scene, AI b-roll for moods stock can't capture. **The slop line:** premium = a real subject (real listing photo i2v, real data) elevated by AI motion/atmosphere, brand-consistent, no warping. Slop = fully-fabricated generic scenes, warped geometry/hands/text, off-brand color, AI narration with no substance. When in doubt, anchor on something real and let AI enhance — don't let AI invent the whole frame.

## 6. Audio strategy (per platform)

- TikTok: trending audio still matters for reach — layer a low trending bed under original VO when it fits, or ride VO-forward for authority pieces.
- IG/YouTube: original VO + generated music bed (ElevenLabs Music) reads more premium and is copyright-safe.
- Always: muted-first design (captions carry it), but reward unmuted with an SFX punch at t=0 and a clean VO+music+SFX mix (see tool-mastery §2).

## 7. Organic-growth mechanics

- **Series compound.** Episodic ("Living in Bend, ep. N", "Market in 30, [month]") trains the algorithm + the audience to expect more. Number them.
- **Profile is the landing page.** Bio = who/where/what-you-get + the FUB-tracked number `541.703.3095`. Handle `@ryanrealtybend` everywhere.
- **Hashtags 2026:** 3-5 specific + local (#bendoregon #bendrealestate #[neighborhood]) beat 30 generic. They're a topic signal, not a reach hack.
- **Post the cut-down where search lives (YouTube/TikTok) and the carousel where saves live (IG/LinkedIn).**
- Measure → double down on what the data says is working (the brain's performance-bias loop does this once channel data is flowing).

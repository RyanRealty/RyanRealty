## Idea
- **One sentence:** A porcelain elk breathes the world into being — everything it exhales becomes the next living thing, until its last breath becomes the elk itself.
- **Formal constraint:** One continuous breath-chain: every new element must visibly emerge FROM the previous element's smoke. No cuts that break the chain. (Schwizgebel's metamorphosis law: decide up front what turns into what.)
- **Trope test:** "Smoke morphing" alone is model-default; the CHAIN with a closing loop is authored structure. Pass, contingent on the loop closing.
- **Filmable test:** Cannot be filmed. Pass.
- **Hang test:** v1's still already reads as gallery-grade sculpture; the risk is the chain sagging mid-piece. Judged at animatic.

## Feeling
- **Target emotion:** Awe (secondary: satisfying, via the loop closing).
- **Mechanism + recipe:** Vastness + accommodation — the scale reveal recipe: start intimate (a nostril, a curl of breath), each becoming is slightly LARGER in scope (breath → bird → flock → forest → weather → mountain), until the reveal that the whole landscape is inside one exhale. Slow the motion at the reveal; one wide beat breathes 3-4s against the swell. Small-self at the peak.
- **Know / Feel / Do:** Know: nothing (no message). Feel: awe, then the click of the loop closing. Do: watch it again (the loop re-opens the gap).

## Lineage
- **References:** Schwizgebel (*The Battle of San Romano* — planned metamorphosis spiral that ends where it began); Miyazaki (ma — the held wide beat); porcelain/blanc-de-chine sculpture tradition (the material's stillness against living smoke).
- **Stealing:** Schwizgebel's decide-the-transform-map-first discipline; Miyazaki's scheduled emptiness; the porcelain tradition's restraint (nothing else in frame is white).

## Sound spine
- **Timing lock:** A single sustained cello note per "becoming," rising a tone each time (6 becomings = 6 notes ascending); breath sound (human, close-mic, slow) is the metronome — one becoming per breath, ~4.5s per breath cycle at rest-breathing pace.
- **Melodic identity:** The 6-note ascent IS the piece's melody; candidate for the brand's recurring sonic asset if it works (marketing-psychology: melody + consistency).

## Anticipation curve
- **Earliest promise cue:** Frame 1 — the elk mid-inhale, smoke already curling from its nostrils (something is about to be exhaled; the promise is in the first 0.4s).
- **The gap opened:** What does the breath become? Re-opened at each becoming (chained curiosity gaps).
- **Peak (≈75%):** The pull-back reveal — the "weather" the breath became is a whole valley's sky, the elk now tiny on the ridgeline. NEW element landing it: the first and only bass note under the cello, synced within 2 frames of the wide.
- **Resolution / ending:** The distant clouds condense back into a white silhouette — the elk — which inhales. Loop point = frame 1. The close re-opens the gap (peak-end: the end IS the peak's resolution; nothing after it).

## Style frame
- **Authored frame 1:** Composite: v1's best still (elk, licensed by us — our generation) regraded to the locked palette; mist repainted for prospect-refuge (forest opening behind the elk = vista through sheltering pines).
- **Audits:** Fluency — one white subject on dark forest, parses instantly. Colors — deep forest greens/navy mist (liked-things: forest, night sky), white subject; brightness mid-low (calm), saturation low-mid (awe is low-arousal-build to high at peak — saturation lifts only at the wide). Curvature — antlers and smoke are all curves. Peak-shift — porcelain whiter than possible, mist bluer than real.
- **Iterations:** v1 still accepted as base (it already hangs); regrade pass = v1.1 before any motion.

- **Budget:** 6 becomings × 3 takes ≈ 18 generations ≈ $15-25; expected keep 6 (33% — generous because start-frames are controlled).
- **Drift log:** maintained in this file during production.

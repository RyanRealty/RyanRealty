# Serious AI Filmmakers — the workflows that separate art from slop

Research date: 2026-07-09. How the AI filmmakers the art world respects actually work. Load before any AI-video generation session.

## Paul Trillo (Washed Out "The Hardest Part", "Thank You For Not Answering", Asteria)

- **Generates ~10× what he keeps:** ~700 Sora clips → 55-56 used; 400+ Gen-2 clips → ~60 used. ([No Film School](https://nofilmschool.com/ai-music-video), [Shots.net](https://shots.net/news/view/how-paul-trillos-short-film-found-the-artist-within-ai))
- **The concept predates the tool:** the Washed Out video was a decade-old shelved treatment; he arrived with the shot progression already designed. ([Forbes](https://www.forbes.com/sites/charliefink/2024/05/07/openais-sora-brings-directors-10-year-music-video-vision-to-life/))
- **Prompts engineered like scripts:** 1,000+ word prompts, "a chemistry formula of words sequenced, timed and paced in a very specific order." Storyboards with stills before motion. ([Rolling Stone](https://www.rollingstone.com/music/music-features/washed-out-a-i-video-backlash-hardest-part-interview-1235017826/))
- **Time saved generating is spent editing:** cuts against VO "sentence by sentence and sometimes word by word."
- **Authorship lives in curation + edit**, and he counteracts the model's pull by citing non-AI influences (Hopper, Wenders, Wong Kar-wai, Lynch) and forcing a period/film-stock grade.
- **Anti-slop stance:** Sora's default look was "a 1990s 3D animation slash stock video look" — he deliberately broke it; embraces "beautiful errors" instead of chasing photorealism.

## shy kids ("Air Head" — the most-documented AI workflow)

- **300:1 generation-to-keep ratio.** Hundreds of 10-20s generations for ~90 seconds used. Three people, 1.5-2 weeks. ([fxguide](https://www.fxguide.com/fxfeatured/actually-using-sora/))
- **Edit-first, documentary method:** treat generations like documentary rushes; find the story in the edit; generate more mid-post to fix pacing.
- **Consistency = prompt discipline + post, not model magic:** hyper-descriptive wardrobe/object prompts, "35mm film" anchors, then rotoscoping, regrade with added grain/flicker, retiming. ~50% of shots got VFX treatment.
- **Sound is human:** voice acting and the band's own song. Unreliable camera-language prompts were faked with crop-pans in post.
- Woodman on slop: "If you only knew how many psychotic balloon-faced things I've seen... It still requires tons of work and tons of molding." ([THR](https://www.hollywoodreporter.com/business/business-news/openai-sora-beta-testers-videos-1235866655/))

## Runway AIFF / AIF winners 2024→2026

- **Daniel Antebi, "Get Me Out" (Grand Prix 2024):** hybrid stack around live action (Luma 3D capture, video-to-video overlay, ComfyUI reskinning). "I don't think I won the AI fest for clever use of the tool, but because the panelists resonated with the storytelling." ([Forbes](https://www.forbes.com/sites/lesliekatz/2024/05/12/runway-festival-creators-of-stunning-top-films-on-ai-as-art-partner/))
- **Jacob Adler, "Total Pixel Space" (Grand Prix 2025):** a 9-minute essay film that won on IDEA, not polish, out of 6,000 submissions with comparable technical quality. ([Artnet](https://news.artnet.com/art-world/total-pixel-space-jacob-adler-a-i-film-festival-2662774))
- **Robert Gaudette, "A Face Only a Mother Could Love" (Grand Prix AIF 2026, Lincoln Center):** 25-30 unproduced scripts written before he touched a generator; built 30+ second continuous sequences against the model's 5-8s window at "an astonishingly high number of generations" — deliberate refusal of the fast-cut slop rhythm. ([THR](https://www.hollywoodreporter.com/movies/movie-features/ai-films-face-mother-love-robert-gaudette-runway-1236624039/))
- **Léo Cannone, "Costa Verde" (WAIFF Cannes best film):** leans INTO hallucinations as memory logic — "emotional re-creations, closer to how we remember than how things actually were." The model's instability becomes the subject. ([Screen Daily](https://www.screendaily.com/news/seven-talking-points-from-the-world-ai-film-festival-in-cannes/5215914.article))
- **AIFF 2025 jury signal:** with generation quality commoditized, the separators are "editing, color grading, sound design, and pacing" — the traditional crafts. ([Spike AI recap](https://www.spikeai.studio/blog/runway-aiff-2025-recap))
- WAIFF artistic director: polish depreciates monthly ("last year's best films wouldn't make the official selection this year") — ideas don't.

## The 10 workflow rules that separate art from slop

1. **Write before you generate.** Every winner arrived with the film already authored. Slop starts at the prompt box.
2. **Budget for brutal overgeneration and curation.** Documented keep-rates: 10% (Trillo), 0.3% (shy kids). Plan the schedule around selection, not generation.
3. **Authorship lives in the edit.** Generation produces material; editing produces the film.
4. **Post-production is mandatory.** Unified grade + added grain/texture + retiming is how disparate generations become one film.
5. **Sound is human and carries the piece.** No winner shipped generated audio as the emotional spine.
6. **Hybridize freely.** Live action, 3D capture, code animation, AI — pure-AI purity is a constraint nobody serious observes.
7. **Force consistency through prompt discipline + post; never trust the model.** Shot-to-shot memory doesn't exist; engineer around it.
8. **Break the default aesthetic on purpose.** Name non-AI influences, commit to a period grade, or make the hallucinations thematic. Unmodified model output IS the house-style slop.
9. **Idea beats polish, and polish depreciates monthly.** Concept is the only durable asset.
10. **Fight the model's rhythm.** Slop cuts every 3 seconds because generations are short. Earned long takes read as authorship.

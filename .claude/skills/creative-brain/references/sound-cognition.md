# Sound Cognition — the science of sound and music for short film

Research date: 2026-07-09. Load when designing the sound spine, scoring, VO direction, or sonic branding.

## The findings

**1. Groove: medium syncopation at ~110-120bpm (High).** The urge to move peaks on an inverted-U of syncopation — medium beats flat AND chaotic (Witek: syncopation explained R²=.35 of movement-urge, R²=.43 of pleasure; [PLOS ONE](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC3989225/)). Optimal groove tempo ~107-126bpm = human spontaneous motor tempo (~2Hz).
→ Score at 110-120bpm with light syncopation; that grid is also where cuts and text-pops land.

**2. Entrainment: attention pulses to the beat (High).** Dynamic Attending Theory (Large & Jones): internal oscillators entrain to a pulse and concentrate attention at EXPECTED moments — on-beat information is processed faster and remembered better.
→ Establish the pulse in the first 2s; place every key reveal on a downbeat.

**3. Audiovisual fusion is mandatory and asymmetric (High).** Synchresis (Chion): sound + simultaneous image weld into one event; "added value" crystallizes at synch points. McGurk proves AV integration is pre-conscious. Binding window: ~50-80ms tolerance when sound leads, 150-250ms when vision leads.
→ Sound lands on-or-slightly-BEFORE the visual hit, never late (100ms late feels broken). Crisp sonic accent on every important cut. Mickey-Mouse one or two motions max — punctuation, not the sentence.

**4. Sonic branding: consistency is the entire mechanism (Med-High).** Melody + spoken brand name audio logos score ~78 vs ~54 missing one; consistent sonic identity lifts recall up to 46%; even Intel's bong only hits 13% attribution — repetition builds the asset (Veritonic Audio Logo Index).
→ ONE 2-4 note ~1.5s signature, same place (end-card) in every video, unchanged for years.

**5. Earworm formula (Med-High).** Sticky melodies: faster-than-average tempo, common rise-then-fall contour, ONE unusual interval (Jakubowski 2017, 3,000 earworms).
→ Familiar arch + one twist for any brand mnemonic.

**6. Psychoacoustics of emotion (High for silence/startle; Med for infrasound/ASMR).** Sub-bass/infrasound pushes affect negative (unease, cortisol) — tension only, never on likable content. A half-second of near-silence before a reveal makes the returning sound hit far harder (anticipation potentiates response). ASMR register (close-mic'd, soft, human, crisp transients — CLOSENESS is the top driver) produces calm + attention together (Poerio).
→ Silence before the punch; close warm foley for trust; sub-bass reserved for dread.

**7. Voice: breathy warmth + living pitch (Med-High).** Breathiness raises warmth; MODERATE pitch variability signals warmth + competence and peaks listener arousal; monotone reads as disinterest.
→ Direct VO for a touch of breathiness and moderate, living pitch movement. (Victoria's locked 0.40 stability / 0.50 style already targets this zone.)

**8. Music assigns meaning to neutral footage (High).** Congruence-Association Model (Marshall & Cohen; Boltz): the same footage means whatever the score says; music heard FIRST shapes interpretation and predictions.
→ Choose the score for the feeling to be ASSIGNED, and set it in the first 1-2 seconds, before the visual subject is clear.

**9. Anticipation is the engine (High).** Huron's ITPRA + Vuust's predictive coding: pleasure = optimal prediction error; pre-outcome tension is where suspense lives; resolution delayed by one beat fires the reward.
→ Set up → withhold one beat → resolve exactly on the key visual.

## The sound design laws (ranked)

1. Fuse sound to the cut — accent every important hit, on-or-before the frame.
2. Score for the feeling you want assigned, and set it first (1-2s).
3. Build tension, withhold a beat, pay off on the reveal.
4. Lock to ~110-120bpm and cut on the grid; reveals on downbeats.
5. Aim for medium, not maximum (syncopation, pitch variation, surprise).
6. Silence before the punch.
7. Never flat, never late (monotone VO; >100ms lag = broken).
8. Breathy warmth with living pitch for VO.
9. One fixed audio signature, same place, every time, for years.
10. Hummable mnemonic: familiar arch, one twist.
11. Close-mic'd soft human sound for trust (ASMR register).
12. Sub-bass for tension only; keep infrasound off likable content.

**The 45s Ryan Realty default:** 110-120bpm bed, light syncopation; emotional cue set by second 2; every reveal on a downbeat with a frame-locked accent; half-second silence before the hero moment; breathy-warm moderately-inflected VO; one unchanging 2-4 note sting on the end card.

# Marketing Psychology at the Root — how feeling builds brands

Research date: 2026-07-09. The serious effectiveness literature (IPA Databank, Ehrenberg-Bass, System1), with numbers. Load when deciding why/where a piece runs, or when anyone (including us) doubts that art-first content is commercially rational.

## The bodies of evidence

**Binet & Field (IPA Databank, ~1,000 audited cases).** Advertising works on two timescales via two mechanisms: emotional brand-building compounds and produces nearly all long-term growth and pricing power; rational activation spikes and decays within ~6 months. Optimal split ≈ 60% brand / 40% activation. At 3+ years, emotional campaigns hit very large profit growth 43% vs 23% for rational. **Fame campaigns beat everything**: very large profit 29% vs 14%, price-sensitivity effects 35% vs 8%. Short measurement windows systematically hide emotion's value (high ROMI, low profit growth — the classic trap). [The Long and the Short of It](https://ipa.co.uk/media/5811/long_and_short_of_it_presentation_final.pdf), [chart compilation](https://thetomroach.com/wp-content/uploads/2020/06/the_greatest_hits_of_binet_and_field.pdf).

**Ehrenberg-Bass / Byron Sharp.** Brands grow by penetration, not loyalty (double jeopardy). Growth = mental availability (linked to Category Entry Points) + physical availability. **Distinctiveness beats differentiation**: buyers don't believe differentiating claims but recognize distinctive assets — and non-verbal assets (characters, colors, sounds, style) beat slogans. **95:5**: ~95% of category buyers aren't in market now; advertising works by planting memory that activates later. Real estate is the extreme case — the winner is whoever is already lodged in memory the day someone decides to move. [95:5](https://marketingscience.info/news-and-insights/advertising-effectiveness-and-the-95-5-rule-most-b2b-buyers-are-not-in-the-market-right-now).

**Orlando Wood — Lemon / Look Out (IPA).** The measured decline in creative effectiveness tracks a drift toward left-brain features. Coded, evidence-backed lists:
- *Effectiveness-sapping (left-brain):* flatness, abstracted body parts, voiceover monologue over product, testimonial, rhythmic beds, obtrusive on-screen text, product close-ups, no sense of place.
- *Fame-driving (right-brain):* characters with agency, dialogue, a clear sense of place, "betweenness" (implicit relationships, glances), humor, wordplay, music with discernible melody, unfolding story, depth of scene, a moment caught in time.
Evidence: 620-ad longitudinal audit + 200-ad UK/US sample plotted against validated star ratings. [A Slice of Lemon](https://cdn2.hubspot.net/hubfs/2235762/Lemon/A%20Slice%20Of%20Lemon.pdf).

**Robert Heath — low-attention processing.** Emotive content is processed and stored implicitly WITHOUT attention, and works better there (no counter-argument). Across 43 TV ads: emotive power predicted brand-favorability shifts; message power showed none. Engagement/recall metrics measure the minority channel. [Heath, Brandt & Nairn 2006, JAR](https://researchportal.bath.ac.uk/en/publications/measuring-affective-advertising-implications-of-low-attention-pro/).

**Zajonc / fluency / Kahneman.** Mere exposure: repetition alone increases liking (works even below conscious perception — "preferences need no inferences"). Fluency is misread as liking, truth, and trust. Consistency is compounding capital; churn spends it down. Caveat: exposure amplifies existing valence — dull or annoying repeated makes it worse.

**Paul Feldwick — Why Does the Pedlar Sing?** Advertising has always worked through showmanship, entertainment, and fame, not private rational argument. Entertainment is not the sugar coating; it IS the mechanism. Permission to entertain, granted by 30 years of the DDB tradition.

**Evaluative conditioning + the pure-feeling proofs.** Pairing a brand repeatedly with affect-laden stimuli transfers the affect (lab-verified, resilient over time). Field proof: Cadbury Gorilla (no message, drumming gorilla, +9% sales, £4.88/£1); John Lewis Christmas (decade of melodic emotional films, £1.2bn incremental sales, up to £10 profit per £1).

**Characters / fluent devices / music (System1, Ipsos).** Recurring fluent devices: +37% likelihood of very large share gain, +30% profit likelihood, 3× the 5-star rate. Character usage collapsed from 41% of IPA entries (1992) to ~4% of US ads — **the best-evidenced and least-crowded tactic in advertising**. Music with discernible melody: +0.7 stars average; rhythmic beds do nothing. [Fluent Devices](https://www.greenbook.org/insights/market-research-industry/the-power-of-fluent-devices), [Listen Up!](https://www.radiocentre.org/wp-content/uploads/2023/10/Listen-Up-FINAL.pdf).

**ESOV + the dull tax.** Growth tracks excess share of voice (~10 ESOV points ≈ 0.5-0.7 share points/yr). Creativity converts ESOV to share up to ~11× more efficiently; **dull advertising needs ~2×-2.6× the media spend for the same result**. Small brands cannot out-spend; they can only out-feel. Earned attention (fame, sharing) is how a small budget generates big effective SOV. [Cost of Dull](https://system1group.com/the-extraordinary-cost-of-dull).

## The 10 laws of feeling-first brand growth

1. **Feeling drives growth; information drives only this quarter** (43% vs 23% very large profit at 3+ yrs).
2. **You're advertising to the 95% not buying yet — build memory, not response.**
3. **Fame beats everything** (profit 29% vs 14%).
4. **Dull is a 2-2.6× media tax you cannot afford.**
5. **Make right-brain work**: living characters, dialogue, sense of place, unfolding story, implicit meaning.
6. **Reach the whole market lightly, not the converted heavily** (~10× ESOV efficiency).
7. **A recurring character is the single highest-leverage creative asset** (+30% profit likelihood, ~4% adoption).
8. **Melody, not just music** (+0.7 stars; feeling transfers via conditioning).
9. **Consistency compounds** — familiarity is misread as liking and trust; never churn the assets.
10. **Don't chase engagement metrics; emotion works without attention** (and short-window ROMI actively misleads).

## What this means for Ryan Realty specifically

- The art-house strategy is not a vanity move — it is the literal evidence-backed playbook for a small local brand: out-entertain inside a tightly defined patch (Bend + feeders) to win effective share of voice creativity can't buy.
- **Jax is a fluent device waiting to be promoted from garnish to engine.** Recur him with agency and personality in every piece.
- Bend-as-a-living-place (Deschutes, Tower Theatre, a specific street) is a coded right-brain feature, not set dressing.
- Pick ONE melodic sonic identity and keep it for years.
- Judge art-house pieces on fame and feeling over years, never on lead attribution windows; run the rational activation layer (LPs, retargeting, CMAs) separately for the 5% in-market now.
- The one-sentence synthesis: **be consistently, distinctively, and pleasantly famous in our patch by entertaining the 95% who aren't buying yet, pairing never-changing assets (character, melody, navy-cream, place) with genuine feeling.**

# Transmission — the science of sharing (and its honest limits)

Research date: 2026-07-09. Why people share, what platforms reward, and what cannot be engineered. Load when planning distribution, loops, or anything meant to travel.

## The findings

**1. THE WATTS LAW — virality cannot be engineered (Highest confidence in the brain).** Music Lab (Salganik/Watts, Science, n=14,341): social influence makes success MORE unequal and LESS predictable; quality only sets floor and ceiling — the same song topped one world and flopped in another. A billion Twitter cascades (Goel/Watts 2015): big hits are broadcast-driven; true viral trees are rare; content features barely predict outcomes. Most cascades die within one hop. Real WOM multiplies reach 1.2-2×, it doesn't replace it ("big seed" marketing).
→ Ship a PORTFOLIO, judge on batting average over ~20 pieces, put paid seed behind winners, and treat any single-video guarantee as folklore.

**2. Completion + rewatch are the ranking currency (High — primary platform documents).** TikTok's leaked Algo 101: heaviest weights on watch time, repeat views, completion. Instagram (Mosseri): completion, re-share, rewatch top the Reels signals.
→ The seamless loop is animation's native advantage: 8-15s pieces where the last frame IS the first = >100% completion before the viewer decides anything. The loop seam is a craft requirement like the safe zone.

**3. People share to look good, not to help you (High).** Impression management is the dominant, mostly unconscious motive (Berger 2014 review); self-disclosure is neurally rewarding — people paid to talk about themselves (Tamir & Mitchell, PNAS).
→ Design the sharer's implicit sentence: "I live somewhere this beautiful, and I have taste." Brand as signature, never subject.

**4. High-arousal emotion travels; sadness stalls (High, with a replication caveat).** Berger & Milkman (7,000 NYT articles): awe +30%, anger +34%, anxiety +21% odds per SD; sadness −16%. Practical utility +30%. CAVEAT: the incidental-arousal study (jogging → sharing) FAILED a 2024 pre-registered replication — content-evoked emotion holds, "raise their heart rate any way" does not.
→ Usable end of the spectrum for a trust brand: awe and warm surprise. Anger and anxiety work and are off-limits.

**5. Triggers beat interestingness for sustained sharing (High).** Berger & Schwartz field data: interesting products spike then die; environmentally CUED, publicly visible things keep getting talked about for months.
→ Anchor pieces to recurring Bend cues: first snow, float season, the Tower marquee, event weekends — the landmark re-triggers the piece every drive-by.

**6. Local identity is a cheat code (Medium-High).** NPR geotargeting field experiment: local posts earned ~6× engagement; top categories were "Place Explainers" (why our town's quirks exist) and local pride. Nostalgia weakens the desire for money and mobilizes connection (Sedikides/Lasaleta).
→ Highest-probability shareable piece in this market: a Place Explainer with a nostalgia layer (the smokestacks' story, the marquee through the decades). This also RESOLVES the Ehrenberg-Bass tension: "everyone who loves Bend" IS the whole category — identity-narrow and reach-broad simultaneously.

**7. Emotional contagion compounds at tiny per-item strength (High).** Facebook contagion is real but microscopic per exposure (d≈0.001-0.008) — it matters as a sustained field across months, not one video's payload.
→ Pick one emotional register and repeat it for months. Consistency IS the mechanism.

**8. Moral-emotional language is the one verified accelerant — refuse it (High).** +13% per word (meta-analysis of 4.8M observations, down from the original +20%), and it spreads by tribalizing.
→ Wrong tool for a brokerage that needs the whole town. Never.

**9. Humor: violate the category, never the community (High as theory).** Benign violation: the joke is on real-estate clichés, HGTV tropes, Bend's roundabout quirks — never on decency or the audience's commitments.

**10. Epistemics law (from the field's own record).** Magnitudes are weather; directions are solid. Berger's arousal study failed replication; moral contagion shrank under meta-analysis; loop "3×" numbers are anecdotes. Distrust any rule with a guaranteed number attached — including ours.

## The transmission laws (ranked)

1. You cannot engineer virality — portfolio + paid seed + batting average (Watts).
2. Completion and rewatch are the currency; the invisible loop is our native advantage.
3. People share to look good — design the sharer's sentence.
4. High-arousal positive travels (awe, warm surprise); sadness stalls; anger is off-limits.
5. Triggers beat interestingness — anchor to recurring local cues.
6. Local identity ≈ 6× engagement; Place Explainers + local pride lead.
7. Tone compounds across months, not per post.
8. Refuse moral outrage, the one accelerant that poisons a whole-town brand.
9. Humor violates the category, never the community.
10. A beautiful piece that also teaches one true, verified local fact gets both the aesthetic share and the utility share (+30%).
11. Distrust guaranteed numbers, including these.

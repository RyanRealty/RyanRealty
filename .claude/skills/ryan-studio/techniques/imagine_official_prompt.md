# Imagine official 30-60 word prompt

Source: round4 #1 (@imagine 2026-09-18)

## Recipe
Template: `Silent, no music. [Subject does an action]. [Camera move]. Maintain the reference image's exact lighting, color and grain. Environmental sound only: [two or three sounds].` One subject, one action, one named camera move. Fix the start frame first. Bend example: `Silent, no music. Late-afternoon sun slides across a sagebrush ridge outside Bend, Cascade peaks soft in haze behind. Slow drone push-in, slightly off-level, subject pushed right of frame. Maintain the reference image's exact lighting, color and grain. Environmental sound only: wind through juniper, a distant hawk.`

## Use
List `imagine_official_prompt` in brief.techniques. Mandatory spec_rules still apply.

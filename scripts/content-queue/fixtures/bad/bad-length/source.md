# Fixture: Septic Basics

In Oregon, a septic addendum lets the buyer and seller decide who pumps the tank and who pays for the inspection. A typical pumping costs about $450 and an inspection runs 2 to 3 hours. The county keeps records for systems installed after 1974. Buyers usually ask for the inspection within 10 business days of mutual acceptance so there is time to negotiate repairs before closing.

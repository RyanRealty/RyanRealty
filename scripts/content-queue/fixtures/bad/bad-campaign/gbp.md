Selling a home on a septic system in Oregon? The tank pumping and the inspection get sorted out in a septic addendum, so you and the buyer agree up front on who handles each one and who covers the bill.

Plan on roughly $450 for pumping, and leave room in the first 10 business days for the inspection.

# Ryan Realty voice

One voice for everything we publish: the site, broker bios, emails, texts, social posts,
market reports, CMAs, and client packets. Set by Matt, 2026-09-07. No word lists, no
punctuation rules, no mechanical gate. If a line sounds like a person who knows Central
Oregon and wants to help, it is right.

## Who is talking

Ryan Realty, a boutique brokerage in Bend, Oregon. Local experts. We pride ourselves on
exceptional customer service. Three licensed brokers who live and work here, and who stay
with a client from the first conversation through closing.

## How we sound

- Warm, direct, and confident. We are proud of the work and we say so plainly.
- Specific beats generic. Name the neighborhood, the price, the thing that matters.
- Talk to the reader. "You" and "we." Write the way Matt talks to a client.
- Helpful first. Every piece should leave the reader knowing something they did not.
- Say what the client gets: quick answers, careful negotiation, current market data, a
  broker who knows the area and picks up the phone.
- Normal English. Normal punctuation. Contractions are fine. Enthusiasm is fine when it
  is real.

## What stays true no matter the surface

- Numbers are real and traceable (CLAUDE.md §0). We never estimate a stat to fill a line.
- Quotes are real. We never write words for Matt, a broker, a client, or anyone else who
  did not say them. Reviews appear as written.
- Fair housing: we describe homes and neighborhoods, never who should live there.
- MLS remarks from other brokers are shown as written.

Common questions

Buying with Ryan Realty

Do I need to sign a buyer-representation agreement before touring homes?

Under the 2024 NAR settlement rules, a written buyer-broker agreement is required before we tour a home together. We walk you through the agreement before your first showing so you know exactly what you are signing and why.

How much earnest money is typical in Central Oregon?

Most accepted offers in the Bend area carry 1 to 3 percent of the purchase price in earnest money. In competitive situations that number can be higher.

How do I get matched to listings without signing up for a national portal?

Submit your criteria through our buyer alert form and a Ryan Realty broker pulls matching listings directly from the MLS.

What areas do you help buyers in?

Central Oregon: Bend, Redmond, Sisters, Sunriver, La Pine, Tumalo, Prineville, Terrebonne, and the surrounding resort and rural communities.

What is the typical timeline from offer to closing?

A standard residential transaction in Oregon closes in 30 to 45 days after an accepted offer. Cash transactions can close in 10 to 21 days.

How does a buyer broker get paid?

In most transactions the seller offers a buyer-agent commission in the MLS. If the seller offers nothing, the buyer-broker fee is discussed and agreed in your buyer-broker agreement before we tour.

About Tetherow

A golf community that put architectural intention first.

Tetherow opened the David McLay Kidd 18-hole course in 2008 and added the Tetherow Lodges resort hotel in 2014. Homesites are clustered into 9 named sub-neighborhoods, each with its own architectural guidelines and lot-size norm: Heath, Tartan Druim, Triple Knot, Lodges at Bachelor View, Westbrook Meadows, Sunrise Village, Campbell Road, Roald West, and 1st on the Hillsites.

Buyers here are typically out-of-state with primary or secondary-home intent. The club is golf-led but membership tiers cover non-golf families. HOA dues run $1,200-$2,800 annually depending on sub-neighborhood; the club is a separate equity membership ($45k-$95k initiation).

HOA + dues reality

What you actually pay each month

Tetherow has two layers: the master HOA (Tetherow Owners Association, ~$1,200/yr) and the sub-neighborhood HOA. Heath adds $1,600/yr. Lodges at Bachelor View runs ~$2,800/yr (covers exterior + snow). The Tetherow Club membership is separate equity, not part of HOA.

None of this is hidden. we'll walk you through every line item, including the master capital reserve study, before you write an offer.

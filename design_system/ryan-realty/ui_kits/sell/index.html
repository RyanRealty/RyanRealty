Our process

What it costs

A straight-up commission conversation.

We charge 5% total on the sale, split 2.5% to the buyer's brokerage and 2.5% to us. That includes professional photos, MLS listing, marketing, all showings, and full transaction management.

If you've heard about 2025's commission changes. yes, we work under the new model. We can show you the exact ALTA breakdown, the buyer-agency agreement we use, and the realistic concession math for your price point. No surprises at the table.

FAQ

A few honest answers

Do I need to sign a listing agreement to get the CMA?

No. The valuation is free, no contract required. If you decide to list with us after, that's a separate signed agreement.

What if my home is in a tiny resort sub-area with very few sales?

For slow-turnover sub-areas like Pronghorn, Crosswater, Vandevert Ranch, Black Butte Ranch. we expand the comp window to 12 or 24 months. We tell you which comps were stretched and why.

How long does it take to list?

From signed agreement to live on MLS is 5-7 business days. Professional photos shot within 48 hours. MLS description and pricing locked the day after photos return.

Will you discount commission?

Sometimes. Every listing agreement is its own conversation. We don't have a published discount, but we'll be straight with you about what's reasonable for your price point.

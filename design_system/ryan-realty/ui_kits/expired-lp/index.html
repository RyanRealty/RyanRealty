First, the obvious thing

Your home was on the market for months. It didn't sell. That's a real outcome to sit with. not a small thing.

Most homeowners we work with felt some mix of frustration, doubt about their prior agent, and confusion about what was actually marketed. All of that is fair. We won't pretend you should be excited about re-listing right away.

The audit

5 things we look at in the free audit

One page, written. No "strategy sessions." We email it within 48 hours of receiving your address.

1

Price thesis vs comps

We pull the closed sales from your immediate neighborhood during your active window. Median $/sqft, sale-to-list ratio, days on market. Verdict: at, above, or below market.

2

Photo + staging quality

Audit of the photo set: hero shot, room coverage, lighting, dated furniture, missing rooms. The photos drove buyers away, or were neutral.

3

MLS description

Did the description name what's unique about THIS home, or use generic words like "stunning, charming, must-see"? Word count, signal-to-noise.

4

Syndication + exposure

Was the listing actively maintained? How often were price changes pushed? How much time between updates? Verdict: actively marketed or sat quietly.

5

Agent engagement

Where the MLS shows showings count + last shown date, we can see if the agent was driving traffic. Above or below the area median.

📄

The verdict

One paragraph at the end: re-list now, re-list with changes, hold, or rent. Sometimes the honest answer is "wait." We tell you which one fits your situation.

Three honest paths

Re-list isn't always the right move.

Sometimes a small price adjustment + a photo redo + a clean MLS description is all the home needed. Sometimes the market moved against you and you should hold a year. Sometimes the home is a stronger rental than seller's market home, especially in Sunriver, Tetherow, or the Lodges-style sub-neighborhoods where short-term rental dynamics are different.

The audit walks each path with specific numbers. projected re-list price band, hold cost per year, expected rental gross. so you can make the call from data, not pressure.

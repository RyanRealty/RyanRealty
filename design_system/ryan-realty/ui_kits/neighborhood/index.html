About this neighborhood

Hilltop custom homes on larger lots

Awbrey Butte rises northwest of downtown. Lot sizes typically 0.5 to 1.5 acres, which is rare for in-city Bend. Most homes built between 1985 and 2010, with a steady trickle of new custom builds on the remaining vacant lots.

Character: quiet, mature pine canopy, no through-traffic. Buyers here are usually trading up from elsewhere in Bend or moving in from California / Pacific Northwest metros wanting a primary residence with view + privacy. HOA is loose by Bend standards. most lots are governed by Awbrey Glen architectural review or similar, with modest annual dues ($300-$1,200 typical).

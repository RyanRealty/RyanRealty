Voice — the principle
Enforced on every surface · web · print · video · social · email
Honest. Transparent. Trustworthy. Direct and kind. We don't tell you we're warm — we just speak warmly. We don't tell you we're expert — we just know the neighborhoods.
Four rules that do most of the work
1
Direct
Short sentences. Active voice. One idea per line. If a clause can be cut without losing meaning, cut it.
2
Specific
Neighborhoods, not "the area." Dollar amounts, not "competitively priced." First names of brokers, not "our team of experts."
3
Kind
Directness is not coldness. "You" and "your." Never condescend, never hedge, never pressure.
4
Honest
Even when inconvenient. Soft market — say so. Listing has been sitting — say so. We don't know — say so. Trust is the product.
Five voice attributes — the bar
TrustworthyEvery claim sourced. Never promise outcomes we can't deliver.
HonestData drives the verdict. If data contradicts the story, the story changes.
KnowledgeableSpecifics over generalities. Define jargon inline. Cite sources.
ProfessionalWarm but never sloppy. No typos. Visually clean.
DependableNever write a CTA we can't honor. Follow through on every promised cadence.

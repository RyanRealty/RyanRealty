Most buyers walk into a builder's model home friendly and trusting. That is natural, and it is also where a lot of money quietly changes hands.

The sales rep is great at their job, but their job is to sell for the builder. If you want someone on your side, get a buyer agreement signed before that first visit. Some builders won't let you bring an agent in later.

A few things I tell every client eyeing new construction in Bend or Redmond:

The contract is the builder's own form, so read the earnest money terms, the materials substitution language, and what happens if the completion date slips.

The base price rarely budges, because it sets the comps for the whole neighborhood. Upgrade credits, closing help, and rate buydowns are where there is real room.

City inspections check code, not craftsmanship. Hire your own inspector before the drywall goes up.

And if the build runs late, your rate lock may expire before the keys do.

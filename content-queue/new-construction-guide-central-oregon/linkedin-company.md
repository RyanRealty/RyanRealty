New construction makes up a big part of what is available in Bend and Redmond, and most of those purchases begin in a builder's model home. What many buyers miss is that the sales rep there represents the builder, not the buyer.

We wrote a practical guide for Central Oregon buyers who want their own representation. It walks through the builder's contract, how upgrade pricing really works, independent inspections during construction, rate locks that can lapse if a build runs behind, and the written warranty offer Oregon law requires. It also covers HOA terms, city development fees, and why we check nearby resale sales so the appraisal supports the price you agreed to.

Sellers in Bend ask me about marketing first. I wish they asked about cost first, because most of it is knowable before you list.

Here is the picture on a $765,000 home, the median sale price in our MLS data. With our 3% listing fee and no cover for the buyer's agent, the sale costs $26,165, or 3.4%. If you agree to cover 2.5% for the buyer's agent, it costs $45,290, or 5.9%.

The biggest swing is that agent pay. Since August 2024, it is no longer bundled with the listing fee. It is not shown in the MLS either. It is a term you decide in each offer, so you should understand the trade before the first offer lands.

The rest is steadier: title, escrow, a lien search, and recording the release of your mortgage. And there is no transfer tax in Deschutes County.

Before you accept anything, you should see a net sheet that shows what lands in your pocket.

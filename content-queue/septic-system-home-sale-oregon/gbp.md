Buying or selling a home in Oregon that has a septic system? The paperwork has a few extra steps. Pumping, inspections and who covers the cost are all settled in a septic addendum (OREF 081) that gets attached to the standard purchase contract. The form lets each side check which services happen, and it leaves the payer choice to you.

Before you write or accept an offer, it helps to talk through who handles what, and how problems found in an inspection would be handled.

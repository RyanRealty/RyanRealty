Your offer price is not the number on the check you bring to the table.

I sit with Bend buyers who budgeted for the down payment and were surprised by everything stacked on top of it. Most of it is knowable ahead of time, though.

Here is the shape of it. Your lender charges for making the loan. A title policy protects that loan. Escrow gets split with the seller by custom. Recording a deed with Deschutes County costs $102 for page one. Then come the prepaids: a year of homeowners insurance, interest through month end, and a slice of property taxes.

Two pieces of good news. Oregon has no state transfer tax, and neither does Deschutes County. And federal rules mean the fees a lender sets cannot climb between your Loan Estimate and the final Closing Disclosure.

If you are using FHA or VA, there are program fees too, like the 2.15% VA funding fee for a first-time user putting less than 5% down. Some veterans are exempt and never find out.

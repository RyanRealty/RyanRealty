Most people I talk to about rent versus buy in Bend are comparing the wrong two numbers.

They set the rent next to the mortgage payment and stop. But owning also means property tax, insurance, and upkeep. On a $733,000 home with 20% down at 6.71%, the full monthly cost lands near $4,825 before insurance. A three-bedroom rental at the federal 2026 fair market figure is $2,481. On cash flow alone, renting is cheaper here today, and I would rather say that plainly than dress it up.

What owning does return is principal paydown, and possibly appreciation. I tell clients to run the numbers at zero appreciation first. If the purchase only works when prices climb, that is a bet, not a budget.

The gap also shrinks on the east side and in Redmond, where the same money goes further.

Rent or buy in Bend? We ran the 2026 numbers using published figures only.

At a $733,000 home price, 20% down, and a 6.71% rate, owning costs close to $4,825 a month before insurance. The federal fair market rent for a three-bedroom is $2,481. So the monthly cash comparison favors renting, and we say so openly.

Owning earns its keep over time through principal paydown and any price growth. We suggest testing your plan with no appreciation at all, then deciding how long you realistically expect to stay.

The guide also covers who should keep renting for now, who is ready to buy, and why the east side and Redmond narrow the gap.

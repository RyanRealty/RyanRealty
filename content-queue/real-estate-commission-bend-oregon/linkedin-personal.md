Most Bend sellers I talk with still think commission is one number that someone else sets. It isn't, and it hasn't been that simple since 2024.

Here is how I explain it. There are two separate fees. The first is what the listing brokerage charges. Ours is 3% of what the home sells for, with no add-on fees. On a $765,000 sale, that is $22,950, and it comes out of your proceeds at closing.

The second is what the buyer's agent receives. Since August 17, 2024, that can no longer be advertised in the MLS. Buyers sign their own written agreement, and as a seller you decide offer by offer whether to cover part, all, or none of it. For scale, each half-point is $3,825 on that same price.

Oregon added a layer on January 1, 2025: a buyer's broker must work under a written agreement that explains how they get paid. No law sets either fee, and both are negotiable.

If you are interviewing agents, ask each one for the fee as a percent and in dollars, what it includes, and how they handle a buyer's request for compensation. Get it in writing.

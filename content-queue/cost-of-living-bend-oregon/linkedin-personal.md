Before you fall for a Bend listing, price the whole life, not just the house.

I put together a cost guide for people weighing a move here, and the pattern is clear. Housing dwarfs everything else. Recent closings through September 7, 2026 put Bend's typical single-family price at $733,000 and Redmond's at $499,000. At 6.71% with 20% down, the Bend figure works out to about $4,214 a month with property tax, before insurance. Redmond lands near $2,869.

The tax picture surprises people in both directions. There is no sales tax, but income brackets run from 4.75% up to 9.9%. Property tax follows assessed value, and Measure 50 caps its growth at 3% a year, so longtime owners often carry a lighter bill than a new buyer would.

My advice is simple. Get a lender payment on a real address, get an insurance quote on that same house, then tour with that number in mind. Rates move weekly, so treat these figures as a September 2026 snapshot.

Your list price is the one seller decision the market answers right away.

Price where buyers are actually choosing and the showings arrive. Price where you hope to land and the house sits, then sells for less after a reduction anyway.

Here is how I think about it with Bend sellers. Closed sales show what people paid. Pending sales show what they are agreeing to today. Active listings are the homes a buyer tours the same weekend as yours. Skip any one of those and the number is a guess.

Then there is timing. A new listing gets its biggest wave of attention immediately, and a price that misses wastes it. The shoppers who passed do not come back on their own when you cut later. Meanwhile the listing starts carrying a days-on-market number that reads like a warning label.

Two more points sellers often miss. Showings without offers are information, and the feedback tells you whether the issue is condition or price. And a seller credit lowers your net just like a reduction does, so I model what you walk away with, not just the figure on the sign.

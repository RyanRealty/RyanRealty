Almost everyone who relocates to Central Oregon starts their search in Bend. Plenty of them finish it somewhere else, and they are usually glad they did.

After years of working with buyers here, I think the decision comes down to three questions. How much home does your budget need to buy? How near do you want to be to the outdoors that pulled you here? And how much town do you want around you?

Bend gives you the deepest selection and the most going on, and you pay for that. Redmond gives you a newer house and a bigger yard for the money, plus the airport, with a drive to Bend of about 25 minutes. Sisters is the small, quiet one. Inventory there is thin, so patience matters, and it is not a bargain option. People pick it for the setting.

If you have never lived through a winter here, renting for a few months before you buy is a sensible test of the commute and the snow.

Bend, Redmond or Sisters? Buyers ask us this constantly, and the honest answer is that it depends on what you want from daily life.

Bend has the broadest choice of homes, the hospital and the most to do. Redmond stretches a budget further, has the regional airport, and sits about 25 minutes from downtown Bend. Sisters is the smallest and quietest, with the mountains close by and a thin supply of homes, so buyers there often have to wait for the right one.

We put together a plain comparison covering commute times, inventory, remote work and whether to rent first. Each section links to the live market page for that town, so you can see current numbers rather than guesses.

Clients who have moved here from elsewhere often ask me the same thing: who pays for the home warranty?

In Oregon the honest answer is, whoever the contract says. Nothing in state law or the licensing rules requires a warranty plan on a sale. It is simply a term the two sides can negotiate.

The standard sale form has a dedicated section. You mark whether a plan is part of the deal, write in the plan and what it costs, and then mark whether the buyer or the seller orders and pays. The printed form leans neither direction, so there is no default hiding in the fine print.

Two things I tell people. First, habits from other states don't travel. If you want the seller to cover it, say so in the offer or in a signed addendum. Second, once the contract is signed, that is the arrangement unless both parties sign a change.

Also worth knowing: the seller's disclosure form asks about warranties on specific building materials, which is a different subject from a home warranty plan. And what a plan actually covers depends on the plan itself, so read it before you rely on it.

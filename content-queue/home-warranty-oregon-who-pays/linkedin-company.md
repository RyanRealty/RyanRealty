Who pays for a home warranty in Oregon? Whoever the sale agreement says.

The state doesn't require a plan, and the standard form doesn't tilt toward either party. It simply gives you a section to decide whether a plan is included, which plan it is, what it costs, and whether the buyer or the seller orders and pays.

If you're coming from a state where one side usually covers it, don't assume that carries over here. Ask for what you want in the offer or in a signed addendum. After signing, the terms hold unless both sides agree in writing to change them.

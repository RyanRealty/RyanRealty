A lot of the people who call me about Bend are coming from California, and their first questions are almost always about money.

Here is what I tell them. Oregon has no sales tax, which feels great at the register. But Oregon does tax income, so the move is a trade, not a free lunch. Have your accountant run both states on your real numbers before you commit.

Closing here carries no transfer tax in Deschutes County. Property tax works differently than what you know: assessed value can only climb a small amount each year, so a home someone has owned for a long time may carry a far lighter bill than the one next door that just sold.

The surprise is insurance. Wildfire exposure across Central Oregon means premiums swing by address, so get a quote on the actual house before you make an offer.

And please visit outside of summer. Summer is the crowded, smoky version of Bend. See it in winter, or at least in spring or fall, so you know what you are signing up for.

Most of my California clients buy before they relocate. We tour by video, move documents quickly, and coordinate a remote closing with the lender and title company.

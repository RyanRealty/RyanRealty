Selling a home with a well in Oregon? The state requires a water test as part of the sale. Once you accept an offer, the seller orders lab testing for arsenic, nitrates, and coliform bacteria, and the reports go to the buyer and the Oregon Health Authority.

The buyer can also ask for extra tests through the standard well addendum, and there is a short window to negotiate if something looks off.

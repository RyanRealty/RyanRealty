If you list a Central Oregon home with a private well, the water test is not optional.

I see sellers surprised by this more often than you would think. Oregon law puts the duty on the seller the moment an offer is accepted. The well gets tested for arsenic, nitrates, and coliform bacteria at an accredited lab, and the reports go to the buyer and to the Oregon Health Authority.

Two things worth knowing. First, the required lab tests cannot be waived, even if a buyer says they do not care. Second, the standard addendum lets the buyer ask for more, like flow, lead, or repeat tests, and the parties settle who pays for each one.

If the results show real problems, the buyer sends a list and both sides get a short window to work it out. If they cannot agree, the buyer can walk away with the deposit back.

My advice to sellers: do not wait. Know your well's story before you hit the market, and talk to a well professional if something looks odd.

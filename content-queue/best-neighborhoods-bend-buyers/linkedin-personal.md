Buyers moving to Bend often ask me for the best neighborhood. I never give a single name, because the honest answer depends on how you live.

So I started sorting the city by the question people actually bring me. Want to walk to coffee and the river? Look at the close-in west side. Want a newer house, a flat driveway, and room for a boat? Look east and southeast. Want a course or a mountain view? Look at the resort ring, and budget for association dues on top of the payment.

One habit helps relocating buyers more than any list. Drive the west and the east on the same morning, then repeat the drive at commute time the next day. Then compare what homes in that district have closed for, not what sellers are asking.

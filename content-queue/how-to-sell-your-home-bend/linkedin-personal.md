Most of what decides a home sale in Bend happens before the yard sign goes up.

Owners often ask me when to call a broker, and my answer is always sooner than feels necessary. The first step is an honest price. It should come from homes that sold nearby, homes under contract right now, and homes competing for the same buyers this weekend. If the figure is lower than you hoped, you want to hear it on day one, not weeks into a stale listing.

The next piece is prep. Buyers here tour several houses in a single day, so they remember what they notice at the door: roof, systems, kitchen, baths, floors, paint. Work that only improves the photos can usually wait. A complete, careful seller disclosure also protects you long after closing.

Then comes the launch. The opening stretch of a listing draws the most attention it will ever get, which is why pricing right at the start matters so much.

When offers arrive, I want clients comparing what lands in their account, not the number in the headline. Credits and agent compensation requests can change the outcome a lot.

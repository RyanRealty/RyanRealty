Selling a home in Bend is a series of decisions, and the earliest ones carry the most weight.

We wrote down the sequence we follow on every listing. It begins with a written valuation drawn from sold, pending, and active comparable homes. Then comes prep focused on what buyers notice at the door rather than what only looks good in photos. After that, a coming-soon period, a live launch, weekly written reports, and offers laid out by the net proceeds you would actually receive.

It also covers inspection, appraisal, and closing, plus how we look after the home when an owner lives out of state.

Our listing plan is 3% with no add-on fees, and the valuation comes with no obligation to list.

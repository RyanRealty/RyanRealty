Bend can look closed off to anyone who has never bought a home. In my experience, it is not. It just asks for a plan.

Here is the short version of what I walk first-time buyers through.

First, get a full pre-approval before you tour anything. The payment your lender shows you, taxes and homeowners insurance included, should drive the whole search. Insurance can vary by address because of wildfire exposure, so ask for it early.

Second, widen the map. Buyers who only look at the west side of Bend often decide nothing fits. East side condos, townhomes and older homes, or a newer house with a yard in Redmond, can change the answer.

Third, protect yourself. Keep your inspection and financing contingencies, and order extra inspections for septic or a well when the home needs them.

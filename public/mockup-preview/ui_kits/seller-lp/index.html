FAQ

A few honest answers

Is this actually free? What's the catch?

Yes. No catch. We do roughly 30 valuations a month and 4-5 of those eventually list with us. That's a fine conversion rate for us. most of the value is in being the broker you remember when you do decide to sell, even if that's two years from now.

How is this different from Zillow's Zestimate?

Zestimate uses a national algorithm with limited local input. It misses lot slope, view corridors, well + septic, river frontage, fire mitigation rating, STR permit status, and HOA tiers. the variables that matter most in Central Oregon. Our valuation is written by a person who has likely walked one of your neighbors' homes in the last 12 months.

Will you call me 50 times after I submit this?

No. We send the written valuation by email within 24 hours. If you want a 20-minute call, you book it. If you don't reply, we don't follow up beyond two emails total. We're not a national lead-gen funnel.

What if my home is in a tiny resort sub-area with very few sales?

For slow-turnover sub-areas like Pronghorn, Crosswater, Vandevert Ranch. we expand the comp window to 12 or 24 months and add adjacent comparable sub-areas. We tell you which comps were stretched and why.

Do you take listings outside Bend?

Yes. We work across Bend, Redmond, Sisters, Sunriver, La Pine, Tumalo, Madras, Prineville, Powell Butte, Terrebonne, and Crooked River Ranch. If your home is outside that radius, we'll tell you who to call.

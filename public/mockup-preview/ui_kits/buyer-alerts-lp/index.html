Why use our alerts

10-minute MLS freshness.

We pull directly from Oregon Data Share's Spark API every 10 minutes. That's the same feed Zillow, Redfin, and Realtor.com use. but their consumer apps batch and delay, often by 4-24 hours. By the time a listing shows up on Zillow, the strongest buyers already saw it on our digest.

If you're hunting a Tetherow, Black Butte, or any resort-community home. the moment matters. The good ones go in days, sometimes hours.

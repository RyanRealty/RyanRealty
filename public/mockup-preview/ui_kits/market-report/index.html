The narrative

What's actually happening in Bend right now.

Under $1M: tight. Median DOM is 32 days and listings priced correctly are getting offers within the first week. The under-$600k segment is essentially out of inventory at 3.2 MoS.

$1M-$2.5M: balanced. Properties priced at-market sell in 6-9 weeks, those priced above sit. Several listings in this band have had 5%+ price reductions in May.

$2.5M+: buyer's market. 14.0 months of supply. Aspirational listings sit for 4+ months. The closed sales in this band are happening at 88-94% of list. If you're selling here, expect a longer timeline and plan for a meaningful negotiation.

About this home

Four-bedroom home on 1.20 acres in the Sunriver Resort community. Built in 1998. Hardwood floors throughout the main level, vaulted great room with stone fireplace, and a kitchen with quartz counters opening to the deck. Primary suite on the main floor. Three additional bedrooms upstairs. Two-car garage with attached storage room.

HOA covers community amenities including the Sage Springs Spa, North Pool, Cove aquatic park, golf at Crosswater and Meadows courses (member rates), and access to 30+ miles of paved trails.

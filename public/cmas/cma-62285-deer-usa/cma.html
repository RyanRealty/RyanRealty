Year-over-year price trend

The cache reports two YoY price readings that point in different directions. Monthly June 2026 median price is up ↑ 4.58% year over year. The rolling 365-day median is down ↓ 1.57% year over year. These two readings are not contradictory. They reflect a market transitioning out of a price-compression period. The monthly figure captures the current month's activity; the rolling figure captures the trailing 12-month trend, which included the prior softening period.

For time-adjustment purposes in this CMA, the rolling 365-day rate of −1.57% per year is used (rounded to −1.5% per year as the more conservative and defensible choice). This is the safer assumption for a seller who wants to avoid leaving money on the table by over-adjusting. It leans slightly in the seller's favor relative to the monthly trend.

What the data means for this listing

  • Bend's "Warm" market (58/100 health score) means well-priced homes sell. The anchor comp at 62315 Deer Trail was gone in 12 days at 98.97% of ask. That is the behavior of a correctly priced, well-presented property.

  • Rural acreage buyers are a specific audience. They are not in a hurry, they do due diligence on COID rights and outbuilding condition, and they will walk if the price exceeds their independent research. A $1,025,000 recommended list is designed to attract this buyer within 30 to 45 days.

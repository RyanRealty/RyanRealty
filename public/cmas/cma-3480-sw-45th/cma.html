Your Home at a Glance

Site & structure

Single-level home built in 2018 in Forked Horn Butte, on the southwest side of Redmond. Three bedrooms, two baths, 1,631 square feet on a 0.21-acre lot with an attached two-car garage. Mid-century modern design with an open floor plan and large windows that frame Cascade Mountain views.

Verdict: a seller's market that rewards accurate pricing

At 3.8 months of supply, Redmond sits just inside seller's-market territory (under four months). Homes priced correctly are going under contract in about 15 days. But the market is measured, not frantic. Health reads "Warm," not "Hot."

The clearest signal for your strategy: 59% of Redmond sales close below the asking price and only 18% close above. The average home settles at 98.9% of list. Buyers are active but disciplined.

What it means for your list price

Price it right and it moves fast. Overprice it and it sits, then sells for less after cuts. Your own 2025 purchase shows the pattern: that sale chased $799,000 down to $650,000 over 105 days.

Year over year, Redmond prices are essentially flat (median down about 2%, price per square foot roughly level). Your home has held its value since August 2025. This is a market to enter at a realistic number, not an aspirational one.

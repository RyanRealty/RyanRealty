What is not adjusted, and why

Bedroom and bathroom counts, lot differences, and condition are shown on the comp table but carry no dollar adjustment in this report. Defensible values for those items require paired-sales evidence specific to each pairing. Where a comp differs meaningfully from the subject on those dimensions, it is weighted down in the reconciliation rather than adjusted with an unsupported number.

Weighting
Reconciliation weights favor comps closest to the subject in living area and closest in time. Weight = size proximity × recency, where size proximity = 1 ÷ (1 + |sqft difference| ÷ subject sqft) and recency = 1 ÷ (1 + months since close ÷ 12). Weights for this set: #1 0.85, #2 0.76, #3 0.73, #4 0.30, #5 0.63.

Development Potential

Zoning of record: MUA10 · Deschutes County (unincorporated). Each answer below is a preliminary read of the code in effect 2026-07-14, with the section we relied on cited. None of it is a land-use decision.

Subdivide or partition · Not available

Below the 10-acre-per-parcel minimum to divide.

MUA10 requires 10 acres per new parcel. This parcel is 2.28 acres.

Source: Deschutes County Code 18.32.040 · deschutescounty.municipalcodeonline.com

ADU · Conditional

A rural ADU is allowed here, with real conditions.

Deschutes County allows one rural ADU outright in MUA10 on a lot of at least 2 acres (5 in some wildfire-mapped areas) that already has a single-family dwelling. The conditions have teeth: sewer or DEQ septic approval before application, wildfire-hardening and defensible-space standards, a size cap, and no vacation occupancy. A rural ADU cannot be used as a short-term rental. This parcel is 2.28 acres.

Source: Deschutes County Code 18.116.355; ORS 215.495 (SB 391) · deschutescounty.municipalcodeonline.com

Second dwelling · Conditional

The rural ADU is the main path. A historic-home ADU is the narrow second one.

Beyond the rural ADU above, a separate pathway exists for lots of at least 2 acres holding a historic home (built between specific statutory dates). Manufactured-home secondary dwellings run as accessory uses in limited cases. Each is its own application.

Source: Deschutes County Code 18.116.350 and 18.116.355; ORS 215.501 · deschutescounty.municipalcodeonline.com

Short-term rental · Conditional

Allowed. The county requires tax registration, not a land-use permit.

Unincorporated Deschutes County imposes no STR land-use permit, density cap, separation rule, or waitlist in rural residential zones. A lawfully established dwelling may rent short-term after registering with the County Tax Office (within 15 days of starting, with a Certificate of Authority displayed in the unit) and collecting the 8% county room tax. Two carve-outs matter: STRs are prohibited on EFU and Forest land, and a property that adds a rural ADU gives up vacation-rental use of both units. Inside a destination resort (Sunriver, Black Butte Ranch, Caldera Springs, Eagle Crest, Juniper Preserve, Tetherow), nightly rental is a built-in resort use, with the resort association layer (rental registration, access fees, quiet hours) governing on top. Confirm any HOA or CC&R restrictions on the specific property.

Source: Deschutes County Code 4.08.071, 4.08.090, 4.08.140 (Ord. 2025-006, effective 2025-09-01); DCC 18.113 (destination resorts); DCC 18.116.355 (rural-ADU vacation-rental prohibition) · deschutescounty.municipalcodeonline.com

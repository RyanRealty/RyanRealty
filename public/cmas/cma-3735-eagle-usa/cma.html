3735 Eagle Rd · Subject & Context

Subject Property

At a glance

End-unit Parkside townhome built by Pahlisch Homes in 2021 inside Petrosa, the master-planned community on Bend's northeast side. The original 2021 builder listing describes it as the "Parkside south end unit townhome with small fenced side yard." 3 bedrooms, 2.5 bathrooms, 1,450 sqft on two levels with a loft, skylights, vaulted ceilings, gas fireplace, quartz counters, luxury vinyl plank flooring, AC, and a 2-car garage. Currently off-market.

  • 3 bedrooms · 2.5 bathrooms · 1,450 sqft

  • 0.05-acre site · small private patio
  • 2-car garage · smart home package · AC
  • Petrosa amenities: pool, clubhouse, fitness, parks, trails

Why this matters

The Parkside plan is the single most-traded floor plan in Petrosa, with 38 closes in the last 24 months. This CMA uses the 8 that decide today's value: all seven 2026 closes plus the most recent prior resale, with 4 active listings and 1 pending as live context. The pricing question is not what a Petrosa townhome is worth. It is where an end-unit 2021 resale sits inside a plan band the market re-prices every few weeks.

Two facts drive the answer. End units carry a verified premium over interior units. And the builder is still selling new Parkside units a few blocks away, which puts a hard ceiling on what any resale can ask.

Listing history

  • 2021-09-30: New construction close at $484,900 (Pahlisch)
  • 2024-05-02 to 2024-07-02: Listed $529,900, closed $529,900 at full price. Offer in 32 days
  • 2024-07 to present: Off-market. No MLS activity in 23 months

What the listing history tells us

The July 2024 purchase at $529,900 ($365/sqft) was a full-price close near the top of this plan's pricing cycle. The market for this exact floor plan has moved down since: Pahlisch's own asks for new Parkside units fell from the $484,900 to $534,900 band in 2024 to $449,900 to $499,900 today, and 2026 resales of 2021-vintage units have closed between $447,900 and $470,000. Bend's overall single-family price per sqft is down 3.3% year over year on a rolling 12-month basis. The 2024 purchase price is a reference point, not today's value. Current value is set by the 2026 comp set on the pages that follow, and this report states that plainly rather than working backward from the purchase price.

Current condition note

3735 Eagle Rd · Pricing Strategy

Pricing Strategy

Three independent methods, one answer. They land within 0.9% of each other, which is as tight as a CMA gets.

Method 1. End-unit tier price per sqft

The three end-unit comps, time-adjusted and normalized for the new-build premium, span $315 to $329 per sqft (3093 NE Nipper at the low end, 3808 NE Suchy at the high end, 3714 NE Petrosa in the middle at $322). Applied to the subject's 1,450 sqft:

$314.78 × 1,450 ≈ $456,400  |  $329.20 × 1,450 ≈ $477,300  |  tier midpoint = $467,100

Method 2. Interior-resale baseline + end-unit premium

Baseline on 3731 Eagle Rd, the purest recent resale data point: same plan, same 2021 vintage, two doors away, closed May 4 at $447,900 ($446,665 time-adjusted). Add the end-unit premium, bracketed between the paired-sales read ($10,000) and the raw resale pair spread against 3714 NE Petrosa ($22,100):

$446,665 + $10,000 = $456,700 (low)  |  $446,665 + $22,100 = $468,800 (high)  |  mid = $462,700

Method 3. Similarity-weighted adjusted-comp reconciliation

The weighted average of all eight adjusted comps from the grid on page 6, weighted by similarity and recency (end-unit resale anchor 25%, next-door resale 20%, recent builder closes the remainder):

Method 3 = $464,200  |  Method spread: $462,700 to $467,100 = 0.9%  |  center ≈ $464,700

Why this list price

About the July 2024 purchase price

The subject was purchased in July 2024 for $529,900, at full ask, near the top of this plan's pricing cycle.

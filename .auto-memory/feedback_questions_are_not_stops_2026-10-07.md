# A question from Matt is not a stop (Matt 2026-10-07)

"dont stop when i ask you a question"

When Matt sends a question mid-work ("is this working", "are we done",
"can we go faster"), answer it and keep the work moving in the same turn.
Do not pause, do not wait for a go-ahead, do not end the turn with the
work parked. Only an explicit instruction to stop, hold, or not do
something is a stop. If a tool call is interrupted by his message, read
the message: a question means resume the interrupted step right after
answering.

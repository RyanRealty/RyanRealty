Our story

Started in 2023 to do real estate the way it ought to be done.

Matt founded Ryan Realty in June 2023 after closing deals at a national brokerage and getting tired of the script. The pitch was always the same: aggressive lead-gen, layered hand-offs, a "team" that meant you actually worked with whoever was on duty that day.

Ryan Realty is the opposite. Three brokers, all licensed and active. The person you meet is the person who closes your deal. We don't sell your contact info to lender lead-buyers. We don't auto-call you. We don't have a "strategy session" script.

We've closed 37 deals in the last 12 months across Bend, Sunriver, Redmond, Sisters, Tetherow, La Pine, Tumalo. We turned down 6 listings we didn't think we could sell well. That's the trade-off.

What we do differently

Four rules we hold to.

Direct.

Short sentences. Active voice. One idea per line. If a clause can be cut without losing meaning, we cut it. You won't get marketing-speak from us.

Specific.

Neighborhood names, not "the area." Dollar amounts, not "competitively priced." First names of brokers, not "our team of experts."

Kind.

Directness is not coldness. We use "you" and "your." We acknowledge the situation. We don't condescend, don't hedge, and don't pressure.

Honest, even when it's inconvenient.

If a market is soft, we say so. If a listing is overpriced, we show you the comps. If we don't know, we say so. Trust is the product.

Meet Jax

Jax is Matt's dog, an eight-year-old black lab who's been to every closing dinner since 2023. He shows up on the yard signs, the postcards, and the Instagram. He's also why most of our brand work feels less like a corporate brokerage and more like a small business in Bend.

If you visit the office on Newport, he might be there.

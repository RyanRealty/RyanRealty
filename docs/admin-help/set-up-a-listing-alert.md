A listing alert emails your client automatically whenever a home matching their criteria comes on the market. You set it up from the client's own page.

## Steps

1. Open the client. Go to **CRM, then Contacts**, search their name, and click the row.
2. Click **Send to contact** (the button in the Website Activity panel on the right, or the one at the top of the page).
3. In the dialog, choose the **Listings** tab.
4. Enter the criteria: city, subdivision, price range, and minimum beds. Fill in whatever should limit the search and leave the rest blank.
5. Pick the alert cadence, daily or weekly.
6. Click **Start alerts + send matches**. This turns on the recurring alert and emails the client the homes that match right now, so the first email lands the moment you set it up.

## Good to know

- The client needs an email address on their record. If they have none, add it first.
- Give it at least one real filter, a city, a price, or beds. An empty search would match the whole market, so the tool blocks it.
- The alert email is tracked. Opens and clicks show on the client's timeline and in their engagement panel.
- To confirm it worked, check the **Saved searches** panel on the client's page, or go to **CRM, then Alerts & reports, then the Listing alerts tab** and search their name.

## Viewing versus creating

The **Saved searches** chip near the top of the client's page only lists the searches already on file, it does not create one. Always start a new alert from **Send to contact, then Listings**.

## Editing or pausing an alert later

Go to **CRM, then Alerts & reports, then the Listing alerts tab**. From there you can change the criteria or cadence, pause, resume, or remove any alert, one row at a time or in bulk. See the article on changing what listings someone gets.

A listing alert emails your client automatically whenever a home matching their criteria comes on the market. You set it up from the client's own page.

## Steps

1. Open the client. Go to **CRM, then Contacts**, search their name, and click the row.
2. Look at the right side of their page and find the **Saved searches** section.
3. Click **+ Add saved search**. A short form opens.
4. Give the search a name the client would recognize, like "Bend under 700K".
5. Enter the city, the price range, and the minimum number of bedrooms. Leave anything blank that should not limit the search.
6. Click **Assign**. The alert is live and tied to the client's email address on file.

## Good to know

- The client needs an email address on their record. If the add form is missing, add their email first.
- New alerts send on a regular cadence. You can change how often from the Alerts & reports page, see the article on changing what listings someone gets.
- To confirm it worked, check **CRM, then Alerts & reports, then the Listing alerts tab** and search the client's name or email.

When a lead clicks a link in an email or text you sent and lands on ryan-realty.com, the site greets them with your photo and contact details, not a generic brokerage number. It happens on its own.

## How it works

- The links in your sends carry a small tag that says which broker the lead belongs to.
- When the lead clicks through, the site reads that tag and remembers it in their browser for **90 days**.
- From then on, the site shows your card wherever it would normally show a broker: on listing pages, in the "Talk to a broker" panel, and on contact forms.

## It routes the lead back to you

If that lead fills out a form on the site, the inquiry is assigned to you, because the site already knows they are yours. Each broker's leads see only that broker.

## Ad links

If you run your own ad, use a **broker link** so the same thing happens for leads who come from your ad. Find them under **Content, then Ad links**: a copy button for each landing page tagged to you. Paste that link into your ad, and every lead it produces is attributed and routed to you.

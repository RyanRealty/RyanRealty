Every number on the dashboard is computed live from your CRM data. Nothing is estimated. Here is what each one counts.

## The five cards across the top

- **New Leads** counts contacts that first came in during the last 30 days. It counts the moment each lead was created, not imports or edits, so a bulk import does not inflate it. The line under it shows how many of those you have not acted on yet.
- **Needs Action** counts automated follow-ups that are paused and waiting for your approval. Each one is a ready-to-send message. They also appear in the Needs your action list further down, where you can send them one click at a time.
- **Tasks Due** counts your tasks due today or coming up. Overdue tasks are not included here, they live on the Tasks page.
- **Appts (30 days)** counts everything on your calendar for the next 30 days: appointments, closings, contract dates, task deadlines, and events synced from Google Calendar.
- **Active Deals** counts your transactions in progress. Canceled, lost, and withdrawn deals are excluded. The line under it is the combined sale price of those deals.

## Everyone versus Just me

If you see the toggle at the top right, **Everyone** shows the whole team's numbers and **Just me** shows only your book. Brokers without the toggle always see their own book.

## If a number looks wrong

The dashboard reads the same records as the rest of the CRM. If a count seems off, the underlying list is one click away: each section header links to the full page where you can see exactly which records are being counted.

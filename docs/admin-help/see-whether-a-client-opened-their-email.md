Every tracked email records opens and clicks. There are two places to look, depending on the question you are asking.

## For one person: their contact page

1. Open the client from **CRM, then Contacts**.
2. On the right side of their page, find the **Email engagement** panel. It shows totals for sent, opens, and clicks, plus when they last opened or clicked.
3. For a specific message, look at the timeline in the middle of the page. Sent emails show their open and click counts right on the timeline entry.

## For alert and report emails: the Alerts & reports page

1. Go to **CRM, then Alerts & reports**.
2. Pick the tab for the email type: **Listing alerts**, **Saved searches**, or **Market reports**.
3. Find the person and read the engagement column. It shows how many emails were sent, how many were opened, how many links were clicked, and the last open date.

## What the numbers mean

- **Sent** counts emails delivered to their address.
- **Opens** counts each time they opened one. A zero after several sends usually means the emails are landing in spam or the address is stale.
- **Clicks** counts taps on links inside the email. Clicks are the strongest signal of real interest.

Every one-to-one email goes out through the composer on the contact's page and is logged to their timeline.

## Steps

1. Open the contact and click **Send Email**. Their address is filled in for you.
2. Pick a template from the dropdown or start with a blank email.
3. Write the subject and body. The **Merge fields** button drops in their name, address, and other details so a template reads like it was written just for them.
4. Attach files if you need to.
5. Click **Send email**.

## What happens automatically

- The email sends from **your own Ryan Realty mailbox** and lands in your real Sent folder, so replies come straight back to you.
- Your **signature** and the **Oregon agency disclosure** link are added to every send. You never paste them in.
- The send is **tracked**. Opens and clicks show on the contact's timeline and in their engagement totals. See the article on checking whether a client opened their email.

## Templates

Save the emails you send often under **Settings, then Templates**, choosing whether to share them with the team. Then pick one from the composer instead of retyping.

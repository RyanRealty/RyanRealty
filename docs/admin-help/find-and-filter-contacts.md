The contacts page is your whole book in one table. Four tools narrow it down fast.

## Search

Type a name, email, or phone number in the search box. The list filters as you type across everyone you can see.

## Smart lists

The left sidebar lists All People plus smart lists. A smart list is a saved filter that updates itself: people flow in and out as their data changes, so a list like "Bend sellers, no recent activity" is always current. Click a list to apply it. Click **New List** in the header to save the filters you currently have applied as your own list, and choose whether to share it with the team.

## Stages

Stages in the sidebar show how many people sit in each pipeline stage, like Lead, Active Buyer, or Closed. Click a stage to see just those people.

## Filters

The **Filters** button on the right opens a panel to narrow by stage, tag, neighborhood, source, and more. The **Columns** button controls which fields the table shows. The scope control switches between brokers if you can see more than one book.

## Acting on many people at once

Tick the checkboxes on the left of the table and a toolbar appears: send a batch email, add or remove tags, export, or delete. Everything applies to exactly the people you selected.

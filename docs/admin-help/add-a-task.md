Tasks are your personal follow-up list. The fastest way to add one is right on the lead you are thinking about.

## Add a task from a lead

1. Open the lead. On the right, find the **Tasks** panel.
2. Type the task in the **New task** box, for example "Call about Bend listings".
3. Press Enter. The task drops into your list with a due date.

The task is tied to the lead, assigned to you, with a due date 24 hours out by default and a type. It shows in the lead's Tasks panel and on your Tasks page right away.

## Work your whole list

Go to **People, then Tasks**. Your work is grouped into four views:

- **Today** and **Overdue** are what needs doing now.
- **Upcoming** is what is coming.
- **Completed** shows the last 30 days of what you finished.

## Reminders

A task due today or overdue can send a reminder text to your phone, if you have opted in to alerts. Ask the team to switch on text alerts for you if you want them.

A CMA (comparative market analysis) estimates what a home is worth using recent nearby sales. There are two ways to start one.

## From the client's page, the fast path

If the contact owns a home and the address is on file, their page shows the home on the right side with the option to generate a market analysis.

1. Open the client from **CRM, then Contacts**.
2. Find their home card on the right side of the page.
3. Click the generate option. The CMA is built from the address on file.
4. When it is ready, the same card offers **Review and Send**. Review it before anything goes out. Nothing sends without you.

## From the CMAs page, for any address

1. Go to **CRM, then CMAs**.
2. Click through to build a new CMA.
3. Look up the property by address or MLS number and enter the client's name.
4. Build the CMA, then review the result.

## Reviewing and sending

- Every CMA lists comparable sales with real closed prices. Check the comps make sense for the neighborhood before sending. You are the licensed judgment in the loop.
- From the CMAs page you can filter by status: draft, finalized, delivered, archived.
- Open any CMA to review it, or download the PDF to print or attach.

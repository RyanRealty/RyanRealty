Texting a contact works the same way as email, and the CRM handles the compliance rules so you do not have to.

## Steps

1. Open the contact and click **Text**.
2. Type your message. The merge-field button works here too.
3. Send. The text goes out from your business line and is logged to the contact's timeline.

## The guardrails working for you

- **Quiet hours.** The CRM holds a text you try to send before 8am or after 9pm Pacific, unless you check the override. This keeps the brokerage on the right side of the rules.
- **Opt-outs.** Anyone who has replied STOP or been marked do-not-text will not receive a message. If someone replies STOP, they are suppressed automatically.
- **Delivery status.** Once the carrier confirms the message, its status shows on the text so you know it landed.

## Replies

Every reply lands in your **Inbox**. Work it top to bottom, reply under the thread, and use **Send and Close** to answer and file in one step. See the article on working a new lead from the inbox.

A deal tracks a transaction in progress. There are two pipelines, Buyers and Sellers, each shown as a board of columns you drag cards between.

## Add a deal

1. Go to **Transactions, then Deals**, and pick the **Buyers** or **Sellers** tab.
2. Click the **+** on the column where the deal belongs, for example Buyer Contract.
3. Enter the property as the deal name, the price, and the commission.
4. Set the projected close date and search for the contact to link them.
5. Click **Create deal**.

The deal is saved with its value, commission, and the linked contact, assigned to you.

## Work the deal

- **Drag the card** between columns as the deal moves from Offer to Pending to Closed. Entering the Closed column stamps the actual close date.
- **Open a deal** to add commission splits, upload files, and track the milestone dates (mutual acceptance, earnest money, due diligence, close). Those dates flow onto your calendar.
- **Deal Reporting**, at the top of the board, rolls up your pipeline by value and stage.

## Good to know

This sales pipeline is separate from the transaction-coordination file, which holds the document checklist and e-signing. Both live under Transactions.

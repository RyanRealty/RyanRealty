The website watches what known leads do, scores it, and surfaces the ones worth calling. You do not have to keep checking.

## For one lead: their page

1. Open the lead.
2. On the right, find the **Website Activity** panel. It lists the pages and homes they viewed on ryan-realty.com.
3. The middle timeline also shows their visits alongside your emails, texts, and calls.

## How a visitor becomes a known lead

The site can only tie browsing to a person once it knows who they are. That happens when the lead clicks a tracked link in an email you sent, signs in, or submits a form. From that point on, their activity attaches to their contact record.

## Hot-lead alerts

When a lead's browsing heats up past a threshold, the system flags a hot lead and creates a **5-minute call task** for the assigned broker. The ones worth calling come to you, so you can act while they are still on the site.

## The live visitors view

The whole stream of recent site sessions, named and anonymous, is under the analytics area for the owner. Day to day, the lead's own page is where you look.

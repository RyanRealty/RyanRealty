Met someone at an open house or got a referral by phone. Get them into the CRM right away so follow-up does not depend on memory.

## Steps

1. Go to **CRM, then New contact**. On the contacts list you can also click the person icon with a plus, near the top right.
2. Enter their name. First and last name is enough to save.
3. Add an email address and phone number if you have them. Alerts and follow-up emails need the email, texting needs the phone.
4. Pick the assigned broker so the new person lands in the right book.
5. Add a note about where you met them or what they are looking for. Future you will thank present you.
6. Save. You land on their new contact page, ready for next steps like a listing alert or a follow-up task.

## After you add them

- Set up a listing alert if they are shopping, see the article on setting up a listing alert.
- Subscribe them to a market report for their area to stay in touch with zero effort.
- Add a task with a due date if you promised to call or send something.

The newsletter goes to your subscriber list, all at once or by segment. Every send is tracked so you can see opens and clicks afterward.

## Steps

1. Go to **CRM, then Newsletter**. The page shows your subscriber counts and every past send.
2. Click **Compose newsletter**. There is also a generate-draft option that writes a first draft for you to edit.
3. Write the subject line. Keep it short and specific, it decides whether the email gets opened.
4. Add preview text, the line inboxes show under the subject.
5. Choose the audience: all subscribers, buyers, sellers, or past clients.
6. Write the body.
7. Use **Send test to me** and read the test in your own inbox. Check the subject, the links, and how it looks on your phone.
8. Send now or schedule it for later.

## After sending

- The newsletter list shows the status of each send: draft, scheduled, sending, sent, or failed.
- Open a sent newsletter to see how it performed.
- Subscribers manage themselves through the unsubscribe link, and you can manage the list under the subscribers section of the Newsletter page.

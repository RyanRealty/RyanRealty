The calendar shows your appointments, your tasks with due dates, and your deal closings together, in day, week, or month view.

## Steps

1. Go to **People, then Calendar**, or click **Add appointment** on a lead's page.
2. Enter the title, date, and time.
3. Pick a **type**, for example showing, listing consult, or closing.
4. Link the contact, and check the box to email them the invite if you want.
5. Save.

## After the appointment

- Record an **outcome** so the appointment shows up correctly in the Appointments report.
- Appointment types and outcome labels are managed by the owner. Ask the team to add one if you need a new option.

## Good to know

- The invite is a real email from your mailbox, not a calendar invite, and there is no client self-booking page today.
- Events from your Google Calendar are counted in the 30-day calendar number on your dashboard.

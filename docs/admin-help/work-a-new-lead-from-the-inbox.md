The inbox is where every text and email conversation lands. Working it top to bottom keeps response times fast, and speed wins leads.

## Steps

1. Go to **CRM, then Inbox**.
2. Check **My Inbox** first. Those conversations are assigned to you. **Company** holds everything unassigned or belonging to others.
3. Switch the list to **Unread** to see only what needs a first look.
4. Click a conversation. The full thread opens with the contact's details on the right: stage, source, price range, and their recent activity.
5. Reply in the box under the thread. Pick **Text** or **Email** depending on how they reached out. You can save a draft if you are not ready to send.
6. When the conversation needs nothing more from you, use **Send and Close** to reply and file it in one step, or close it from the header.

## Unknown callers

If the conversation is from a number the CRM does not recognize, the right panel offers **Add person**. Enter their name and email to create a proper contact, or link the number to someone who already exists.

## Tips

- Leave an internal note in the note tray at the bottom of a thread. Notes are visible to the team, never to the client. Mention a teammate with @ and they get an email.
- Select several conversations with the checkboxes to close or assign them in bulk.
- Closed conversations are not gone. They reopen automatically if the person writes back, and you can find them in the Closed folder anytime.

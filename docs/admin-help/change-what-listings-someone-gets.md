When a client's search changes, edit their alert instead of creating a new one. Their history and engagement stay attached.

## Steps

1. Go to **CRM, then Alerts & reports**.
2. Stay on the **Listing alerts** tab. If the person signed up on the website themselves, they may be under **Saved searches** instead.
3. Search by their name or email to find the row.
4. Click the **three-dot menu** at the end of the row and choose **Edit**.
5. Change what you need: the name, the email frequency (daily or weekly), the city, the price range, or the minimum beds and baths.
6. Save. The next alert email uses the new criteria.

## Related actions in the same menu

- **Preview email** shows exactly what the client receives.
- **Pause** stops the emails without deleting the alert. **Resume** turns them back on.
- **Delete** removes the alert for good. It cannot be undone.

## Bulk changes

Tick the checkboxes on several rows and a toolbar appears with **Pause**, **Resume**, **Set frequency**, and **Delete** for everything selected at once.

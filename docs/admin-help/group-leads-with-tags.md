Grouping is how you act on many people at once, and how automated follow-up decides who to enroll. You group with tags and with smart lists.

## Tag a lead

1. Open the lead. On the left, in the **Details** section, find the **Tags** row.
2. Click the **+** and start typing. Matching tags appear, like "Audience - buyer" or "Seller".
3. Click a tag to apply it, or type a new name and choose **Create new tag**.

The tag is saved to the lead. Tags are what smart lists, newsletter segments, and automated follow-ups all match on, so a tagged lead flows into the right groups everywhere in the CRM. Picking an existing tag stores its exact system value, so a lead you tag "Audience - buyer" is worked by the same buyer automations as a lead who came in through a buyer landing page.

## Build a smart list

A smart list is a saved filter that updates itself. People flow in and out as their data changes, so a list like "Bend buyers, no recent activity" is always current.

1. On the contacts page, open **Filters** and narrow by stage, tag, neighborhood, source, or price.
2. Click **New List** in the header to save those filters as your own list.
3. Choose whether to share it with the team.

## Act on a whole group at once

Tick the checkboxes on the left of the contacts table and a toolbar appears. You can send a batch email, add or remove tags, put people on a saved search or a market report, enroll them in a follow-up sequence, set a source, or export. Everything applies to exactly the people you selected, and the CRM tells you how many will be affected before it runs.

Market reports are recurring emails with real sales data for a chosen area. Subscribing a client keeps you in front of them with something genuinely useful.

## Steps

1. Open the client from **CRM, then Contacts**.
2. Near the top of their page, find the quick-action chips and click **Market reports**. You can also scroll the right side of the page to the **Market reports** panel.
3. Pick the areas they care about, for example Bend or Redmond. You can select more than one.
4. Pick how often the report goes out: weekly, monthly, or quarterly.
5. Save. The subscription starts with the next send for that cadence.

## Managing report subscriptions later

- To see everyone who receives reports, go to **CRM, then Alerts & reports, then the Market reports tab**.
- From that table you can search by name or email, change frequency, pause, resume, or remove subscriptions, one row at a time or in bulk with the checkboxes.
- The engagement column shows sends, opens, and clicks, so you can tell who actually reads them.

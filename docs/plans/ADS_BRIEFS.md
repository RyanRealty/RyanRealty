## Compliance guardrails (BOTH — non-negotiable, verified 3-0)
- **Describe the PLACE and the OFFER, never the occupant.** BANNED regardless of intent: "perfect for families / retirees / young professionals / first-time buyers," any age / familial-status / demographic framing. (Fair Housing + brand voice both demand this.)
- **No selectively one-demographic human models.** Show the place, the homes, the broker — not a cast.
- **No "won't last / act fast" FOMO** (refuted 0-3). Lead with access, real numbers, honesty.
- Brand voice: show don't tell, a number beats an adjective, no hype/cliché words.

## BUYER AD — "This is Bend. Want first look?"
**Sell the life; the listing alert is just the easy first step.** (The Welcome-to-Charlotte / Jered-Jones / Madison-Sutton lifestyle model, Bend edition.)

- **Who it's for:** the high-performing metro professional sick of the commute/grind, chasing a higher quality of life — outdoors, health, tribe, and a better place to raise their kids. **The spine is THE TRADE: what they left vs. what's here.** (Sell the place + the life; never name the occupant — see the fair-housing trap.)
- **Format:** 20-30s **vertical video (9:16)**, built on our OWNED 4K masters. Authentic > glossy.
- **Emotional arc (mapped to footage we own):**
  - **0-3s HOOK — the trade, full force:** Mt Bachelor Twilight / Aerial or Sparks Lake Reflection (our 4K), sound-off text overlay: **"Trade your commute for this."** / alt **"This is the drive to work in Bend."** The push→pull contrast IS the scroll-stop.
  - **4-22s — the life, made visible:** Sparks Lake paddleboarding (before the workday), Mountain Bike on the pine trail (lunch break), the Deschutes, golden hour — each with a contrast/number overlay, never an adjective: *"First chair, then your 10am."* · *"20 minutes to the water."* · *"The trail is your gym."* This is the outdoors / health / activity / tribe — the quality of life they're moving toward.
  - **23-30s — the soft turn:** "Homes here move fast. See them before they hit Zillow." + CTA.
  - **Throughline:** not "buy a house" — *"this could be your life."*
- **Hook lines (sound-off, fair-housing-safe):** "Trade your commute for this." · "This is a Tuesday in Bend." · "Your morning, reimagined."
- **Headlines (pick/test):** (1) "Central Oregon listings — sent within 30 minutes of going live." (2) "See Bend homes before they hit the public sites." (3) "Early-access + off-market alerts for your price range."
- **Primary text A:** "Homes in Central Oregon move fast. Get new and early/off-market listings sent to you within 30 minutes — matched to your price range and must-haves. Set it up in under a minute; unsubscribe anytime."
- **Primary text B:** "Want first look at Bend-area homes? We send new + coming-soon listings within 30 minutes of hitting the market — scoped to your budget and the one feature you won't compromise on. Free, no spam."
- **Offer:** new + early/off-market listing alerts within 30 min (→ `/lp/buyer-listing-alerts`).
- **Instant form (Higher Intent):** Q1 "How soon are you looking to buy?" (0-3 / 3-6 / 6-12 / 12+ mo) · Q2 "Do you need to sell before you buy?" (Yes / No / First-time buyer) · Q3 (opt) "Pre-approved or working with a lender?" (Yes / Not yet / Need a referral).
- **CTA:** "Sign Up."

## SELLER AD — "The honest read on your home's value"
**Trusted advisor, not "list now."** They're weighing the biggest financial move of their life. Be the calm, credible local source with real numbers. (McGovern/Parker market-truth model + the verified lowest-CPL "what's your home worth" offer.)

- **Format:** 20-40s **vertical video, BROKER ON CAMERA** (Matt / Rebecca / Paul — the face IS the trust signal; 73% of homeowners are more likely to list with a video agent). Phone-shot authentic is on-brand.
- **Shot list:**
  - **0-3s HOOK:** broker to camera, one credible line (sound-off text mirror): "If you bought in Bend before 2022, you probably have more equity than you think." (Name-and-reframe; a number beats an adjective.)
  - **4-30s:** broker demystifies ONE local stat + the net-proceeds idea — "Here's what Central Oregon homes are actually doing this month, and what you'd *net* after fees — real numbers, no pressure." Cut to a real local street/home (NO models). Honest, specific, calm. (Hyper-local only — national content kills local conversion.)
  - **31-40s:** soft ask — "Want the honest read on yours? I'll send a free equity report — what it'd sell for now and what you'd walk away with. No obligation." + CTA.
- **Headlines (pick/test):** (1) "What's your home worth in [neighborhood] this month?" (2) "Bought in Bend before 2022? See your equity." (3) "Your home's value + what you'd net after fees."
- **Primary text A:** "Bought your home in Central Oregon before 2022? You may have more equity than you think. Get a free, no-obligation Home Equity Report showing what your home could sell for right now — plus an estimate of what you'd walk away with after fees. Takes 60 seconds."
- **Primary text B:** "Curious what your [neighborhood] home is worth this month? Get a free CMA with a real price range and a 7-day prep plan — real numbers from a local broker, not an automated guess."
- **Offer:** free personalized **Home Equity Report / CMA + net sheet emphasizing NET PROCEEDS** (not a vague Zestimate); optional 7-day prep plan (→ `/lp/seller-home-value`).
- **Instant form (Higher Intent):** Q1 "How soon are you thinking of selling?" (0-3 / 3-6 / 6-12 / 12+ mo) · Q2 "Did you buy your current home before 2022?" (Yes / No) · Q3 (opt) "Also planning to buy a new home?" (Yes / No / Not sure).
- **CTA:** "Get Quote."

## Production + ops
- **Buyer video:** edit fresh Central Oregon lifestyle b-roll (asset-library raw footage + third-party vendors) to a NEW concept. No reuse of old renders.
- **Seller video:** broker on camera (each broker shoots their own 30s cut → personal trust). Light local b-roll, no models.
- **Per-broker:** each broker runs their own cut — for traffic ads append `?agent=<slug>`, for FB lead-form ads name the campaign with their first name (e.g. "Bend Seller — Rebecca"). Routing is already wired both ways.
- **Form type:** "Higher Intent" (adds a review step that filters junk). 1-3 qualifier questions max.
- **Fatigue:** refresh creative every ~2-3 weeks; rotate 2-3 hook variants per ad from launch (don't "score later").
- **Measure:** cost-per-QUALIFIED-lead + speed-to-first-touch (minutes). Ignore in-window ROAS.

# Real-Estate Ad Swipe File — what the pros actually run

**2026-06-23, verified across 2025-2026 sources.** Concrete, named examples + the exact hooks. This is the benchmark for Ryan Realty's buyer + seller ads. The through-line: **the scroll stops in the first 3 seconds, readable with sound OFF, and the agent's face/voice or a withheld number does the selling — not a feature list.**

---

## BUYER — sell the place + the feeling (lifestyle)

1. **"Welcome to Charlotte NC" (Steve)** — a destination channel branded entirely around *moving to the city*, not listings: neighborhood deep-dives, "NC vs SC," school districts, honest pros/cons. **Hook = the literal search query** ("Moving to Charlotte? Everything to know before you move"). Relocation buyers binge this months before they ever call an agent → inbound, pre-trusting. → **This is the model for "Moving to Bend / Living in Sunriver."** [src](https://www.labcoatagents.com/blog/winning-the-relocation-game-how-to-dominate-out-of-state-buyers-using-youtube/)

2. **Jered Jones (Las Vegas)** — a spontaneous, *authentic-reaction* home/neighborhood tour; he fell for the home on camera and bought it himself. **Hook = raw unscripted enthusiasm** ("wait — look at this…"). ~1M views in 2-3 days → direct "I want to work with you" DMs. **Authenticity > polish.** [src](https://www.inman.com/2025/09/09/how-this-agent-went-viral-by-falling-in-love-with-the-home-in-his-tiktok-video-tour/)

3. **Madison Sutton (@thenycagent, BHS)** — "apartment tour as lifestyle reel." **Hook = "Here's what $X/month gets you in [neighborhood]"** — leads with the number + place so buyers self-qualify. One reel → 1.5M views → got recruited by the brokerage. [src](https://eliteagent.com/madison-sutton-how-to-ace-social-media-and-build-a-global-following/)

4. **Relocation-channel playbook** (Relocating to Raleigh, Lira Group Nashville, FL "Living in" agents) — six evergreen formats: *Moving to / Living in / Pros & Cons / Best Neighborhoods / Cost of Living / What $X gets you in [City]*. A grocery-bill cost-of-living breakdown drove inbound calls in weeks. [src](https://coffeecontracts.com/blog/instagram-for-real-estate-agents-what-s-actually-working-in-2025)

5. **Brad Ziemer — 5 visual hooks** (the *first frame* is the scroll-stopper, no words): Moving (start mid-motion), Popping (quirky reveal), **Drone** (aerial of size/acreage), Welcome (front-door vibe), **Lifestyle** (a "feeling" shot). Match the hook to the property. [src](https://www.tiktok.com/@bradziemer/video/7519507509439188238)

## SELLER — be the trusted advisor (not "list now")

6. **Bern McGovern — "Living in San Diego" monthly market report** — translates one jargon stat into plain English ("I'll explain Months of Supply and what it means for YOUR home's value"), alternating data with client stories. **73% of homeowners are more likely to list with an agent who uses video.** [src](https://inboundrem.com/housing-market-report-videos/)

7. **Dan Parker — "the REALITY of the market"** — **name-and-bust the fear**: "Everyone's telling you the market crashed. Here's the REALITY of what the numbers say…" Always closes with "here's what it means if you're a buyer, AND if you're a seller" → signals he's not biased toward getting the listing, which earns the listing. [src](https://inboundrem.com/housing-market-report-videos/)

8. **The Loken Group / Whissel Realty — "What's your home worth?"** — the evergreen seller-lead workhorse. **Hook = "Curious what your [neighborhood] home is worth? Free, no-obligation report."** + native Instant Form. **Cheapest qualified seller-intent lead in the channel (~$8-$35 CPL).** Equity curiosity is a near-universal homeowner trigger. [src](https://www.saleswise.ai/blog/real-estate-facebook-ad-examples)

9. **Jeremy Knight — hyper-local zip-code reports** — "Let's look at exactly what's happening in [zip] right now — the numbers and the actual listings behind them." Granularity = irrefutable proof of local mastery. [src](https://inboundrem.com/housing-market-report-videos/)

10. **Glennda Baker (@glenndabaker, 1M+)** — story-first confessional reels: **"That time I made $137K on one sale…"** — the payoff/promise is the first sentence. Batch-shoots 1 day/month → 50-60 videos → posts daily. 8 deals / $141K GCI traced to it. Personality, not the house, sells the listing appointment. [src](https://www.homelight.com/blog/agent-glennda-baker-tik-tok-real-estate-podcast/)

## HOOKS & MECHANICS (reusable for both)

11. **"Guess the Price"** — the single highest-engagement format on TikTok. **"How much do you think this costs? Comment before you watch to the end."** Structure: exterior → 3-4 hero interiors → **price card LAST**. The withheld number forces completion + floods comments (the two signals the algorithm rewards). ~10x normal engagement. [src](https://coffeecontracts.com/blog/the-ultimate-guide-to-viral-listing-reels-what-to-post-as-a-realtor-instead-of-just-sold)

12. **BAM's 6 verbal hooks** — stack a spicy first line over a quiet visual action: Admission ("I was dumb so you don't have to be"), Controversial ("If this offends you, it was meant for you"), Disrespectful, Tough-Love ("brace yourself, this is too honest"), Direct Callout, Curiosity ("Bad news for me, good news for you" → a price drop). [src](https://nowbam.com/6-viral-hooks-every-realtor-should-be-using-right-now/)

13. **Personality > polish (Matt Lionetti, Mike Hege)** — relatable/comedy to-camera, raw phone aesthetic, sound-off-readable text overlay. Hege's "taking deep breaths" hit 85M views with zero budget. Builds parasocial trust so the client "already knows you" before the first call. [src](https://nowbam.com/the-funniest-real-estate-videos-of-2025-according-to-agents/)

14. **Whissel's rule: different audience → different creative/channel** — never one catch-all ad. Run **buyer urgency** ("✨ Just Listed in [neighborhood]") separately from **seller equity** ("Free Home Value Report"). [src](https://www.saleswise.ai/blog/real-estate-facebook-ad-examples)

## The benchmark numbers (gates)
- Sub-60s vertical video → **~2.5x more shares** than longer; **Reels ~67% more engagement** than static; real-estate Reels carry the **highest IG engagement (~3.7%)**.
- **Instant Forms beat landing pages ~2-3x on conversion, ~50% lower CPL** (confirms our plan).
- Seller home-value instant-form leads **~$8-$35**; blended FB real-estate CPL **~$17-$37**.
- **Keep it hyper-local** — Malcolm Lawson scaled a market-update channel nationally and *lost* local conversion. Bend/Sunriver/Central Oregon only, never generic national content.

# Ads — Creative Direction (the North Star)

**Matt directive 2026-06-23.** The old ads were boring and lame. We are starting from SCRATCH. Do not reuse the old format. These ads MUST convert. We have every component to make highly compelling ads and will not settle for less. This document is the creative brief the research + production work must execute against.

## The core insight
We are not advertising listings or forms. We are selling two things:
- **To buyers: a feeling** — the Bend lifestyle and quality of life.
- **To sellers: trust** — be their trusted source for real-estate information as they plan a major life move.

## THE BUYER (the person we're actually talking to)
A high-performing professional in a crowded metro who has hit the ceiling of what the city gives back. **The push:** the daily commute, the gridlock, the concrete, the time tax, the disconnection — the quiet realization that the "successful" life they built is costing them their days, their health, their presence. **The pull:** a higher quality of life — the outdoors, health, activity, and a *tribe* (real community, being known) — and the deeper one: **raising their kids somewhere better** (a wholesome, active, connected childhood). Remote work made it possible; now it's a values decision with a touch of urgency — *life's too short, the window's now.*

The insight that makes the ad work: **they are not buying a house. They are buying a different life — and a different version of themselves and their family.** Trade the commute for the trail, the screen for the river, the crowd for a community, the gray for the light. The job of the ad is to make them *feel* that life and see themselves in it.

### ⚠️ The fair-housing trap (must thread this)
The "raise their kids somewhere better" motivation is **familial status — we can NEVER target it in copy.** No "perfect for families," "great for raising kids," "for parents/professionals," no demographic targeting. We honor that motivation *implicitly* by showing the wholesome, active, outdoors, connected life — and the right person sees themselves and their family in it. **Sell the PLACE and the LIFE; never name the occupant.**

## The deeper truth + our position (Matt directive 2026-06-23) — this is the ad's soul
- **The real pain isn't the commute. It's the quiet fear they're MISSING THEIR LIFE.** The years slipping by in traffic, meetings, screens, gray — the sense that "success" bought them everything except *actually living*. That FOMO-on-your-own-life is the gut driver under every lifestyle shot.
- **Our position is lived empathy, not a pitch: WE WERE THEM.** We felt that exact thing. We made the move to Central Oregon. So the ad speaks **peer-to-peer, not agent-to-prospect**: *"We get it — we were you. And you're not wrong."* We **validate the instinct** instead of manufacturing urgency. That authenticity — a real person who made the leap — is the trust that makes the whole thing land, and it's exactly what the swipe file proves converts (lived story + personality beat polish).
- **Voice / throughline:** affirm the instinct, then show it's real and within reach. Not "look at this pretty place" — **"you already know you want this. We did too. It's a Tuesday here. Come live it."** A light broker-voice or first-person text thread over the lifestyle montage carries it. Never salesy, never hype — Matt's honest, direct, kind register.
- **The emotional turn the ad must hit:** *missing out → seen ("we were you") → validated ("you're not wrong") → it's real and it's here → the easy first step.*

## BUYER AD — sell the life, not the listing
- **Format: VIDEO.** Motion, place, emotion. Not a static price card.
- **What we're selling:** the Bend / Central Oregon lifestyle and quality of life — the Deschutes, the trails, the Old Mill, the light, the pace, the "imagine waking up here." The home is the doorway; the life is the product.
- **Emotional job:** aspiration + belonging. Make a scroller stop and *want* to live here, then offer the easiest first step (early/off-market listing alerts in 30 min).
- **Assets:** lifestyle video from our asset library AND third-party vendors (stock/drone/lifestyle b-roll). Real Central Oregon footage > generic.
- **Conversion:** the feeling earns the click; `/lp/buyer-listing-alerts` or an instant form captures it. Speed-to-first-touch closes it.

## SELLER AD — be the trusted advisor, not the pushy lister
- **The seller's reality:** they're contemplating the next step in a larger life plan that may involve selling their largest asset. This is weighty, not impulsive. They are gathering information and deciding who to trust.
- **What we're selling:** Ryan Realty as the **trusted source for real-estate information** — direct-broker accountability, real local data, no pressure. We help them understand their position before they ever commit.
- **Emotional job:** confidence + safety. Lower the stakes of "just looking," position us as the honest expert who gives them the data (what their home is worth, what the market is doing) with no hard sell.
- **Tone:** calm, credible, specific. A number beats an adjective. The opposite of "List now! Act fast!"
- **Conversion:** the trust + the data offer (instant home valuation / CMA at `/lp/seller-home-value`) earns the lead; the follow-up nurtures the longer decision.

## EXCLUDED — do not reuse
- **Every video/reel we have already produced** (the `v5_library` renders, listing reels, market/news clips) is OFF the table. Matt's call 2026-06-23: they're boring and lame; they will NOT be used. Do not "seed" from them, do not adapt them. Start from a blank page.
- Usable video = FRESH footage: raw lifestyle/drone b-roll from the asset library + third-party vendors (stock/cinematic), edited to a pro-caliber NEW concept — not our old finished ads.
- Benchmark against what top real-estate marketers actually run (see `ADS_PRO_EXAMPLES.md`), not our past output.

## Non-negotiables (apply to both)
- Brand voice: show don't tell, a number beats an adjective. Banned: stunning, dream home, nestled, must-see, act fast, and the rest of the cliché/hype list.
- Housing Special Ad Category compliant — no fair-housing-risk or discriminatory framing (no "perfect for families," lifestyle targeting by protected class, etc.). Sell the PLACE, not a demographic.
- Highly compelling or it doesn't ship. The bar is "stops the scroll + earns the lead," not "checks the boxes."

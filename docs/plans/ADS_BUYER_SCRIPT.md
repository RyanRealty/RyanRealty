# Buyer Ad — "A Tuesday" (30s vertical, draft v3)

One ordinary weekday in a life worth living — told in timestamps over real Central Oregon footage. **No broker, no voiceover, no pitch, no logo until the last frame.** The viewer does the math (*…that's not my Tuesday*); the finite clock does the rest. These are substantive people in an existential crisis about missing the important things — they read restraint as respect and a pitch as an insult. Show, don't tell. A number beats an adjective; here the numbers are the clock.

## The rule that makes it work
**No broker. No face, no voice, no "we," no logo-shout.** It is a short film about a life until the final frame. The only allowed words are the timestamps + the one finite-time beat (`You only get so many.`) — and cut even that if it feels sentimental. The contrast with the viewer's own Tuesday — which we never show — carries the entire emotional load.

## Production notes
- 9:16, 30s, ~4s/beat, ambient sound design only (no on-the-nose music swell). Timestamps in the caption-safe zone, sound-off readable.
- **Per-broker:** no broker on screen — routing is link-only (`/lp/buyer-listing-alerts?agent=<slug>`, or the broker's name in the FB campaign). The film never changes.
- Banned: any broker/agent/logo-forward moment, narrated emotion, hype/cliché words, "family/kids/perfect-for," fake urgency. The footage and the clock do everything.

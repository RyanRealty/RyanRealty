## Alt hooks (A/B the first 3s)
- "Trade your commute for this."
- "This is a Tuesday in Bend."
- "You're not crazy for wanting out."

## Why it stops the scroll (not a tourism reel)
It's a **peer who made the leap** telling a stranger their instinct is right — over footage that's unmistakably real Bend. The turn from *missing out* → *"we were you"* → *"you're not wrong"* → *"it's a Tuesday here"* is the emotional payload; the offer is just the easy first step at the end.

## Footage map (almost all ours)
- **Own (4K masters):** Mt Bachelor twilight, Sparks Lake paddleboard, Mountain Bike, Deschutes/kayak, Old Mill golden hour. → the spine.
- **Source (2 clips):** Smith Rock climber (Getty — real Smith Rock climbing exists), brewery patio with friends (Getty/Artgrid — needs model + property releases since people are recognizable).

## Production notes
- 9:16 vertical, 30s, fast cuts (3-4s/beat), one continuous emotional build. Trending-but-restrained audio under the VO; VO carries it.
- On-screen text in the caption-safe zone, sound-off readable (most watch muted), Amboqia per brand.
- Per-broker cut: each broker records their own VO + uses their `?agent=` link / campaign name. Their voice = their trust = their leads.
- NO hype words, NO "family/kids/perfect for," NO fake urgency. Validation, not pressure.

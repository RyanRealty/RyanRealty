# Buyer Ad — "A Tuesday" (30s vertical, draft v2 — restrained)

**v1 was too overt** (narrated the emotion). v2 shows, doesn't tell. The device is **time-stamps over real footage** — a single ordinary weekday, and what fits inside it. No emotional voiceover, no "we were you." The viewer does the math themselves (*…that's not my Tuesday*), which lands harder than any line. The "we get it / you're not wrong" soul is carried by the *restraint* and the unmistakably-local footage — not stated. A number beats an adjective; here the numbers are the clock.

## Why restraint is the premium signal
The whole story is told in five timestamps and real footage. The contrast with the viewer's own Tuesday (the one we never show) does the emotional work. No hook line explaining the trade, no VO arc, no "dream/lifestyle" words. Confidence reads as premium; over-explaining reads as a pitch.

## Alt opening beats (A/B the first 4s — all oblique, none state the trade)
- `6:41 AM` over the dawn paddle (recommended).
- A single held shot of Smith Rock with `30 min from your door` (a place + a number).
- Mt Bachelor first light with `Tuesday.` only.

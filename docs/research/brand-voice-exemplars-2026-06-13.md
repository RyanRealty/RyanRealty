# Brand voice research — calibrating Ryan Realty (2026-06-13)

Matt: "research other highly effective brand voices, i dont feel like im doing this correctly."
Finding: Matt's stated instincts (authentic, no pander, assume clients are smart, educate
without talking down, never overtly state virtues, not there to make a sale, restraint)
are the exact philosophy of the most-respected restrained brand voices. He is not doing it
wrong — the homepage COPY kept violating his own correct voice by telling instead of showing.

## Two camps; Ryan Realty is "smart/restrained," not "loud/irreverent"
RESTRAINED (Ryan Realty's lane):
- Basecamp / 37signals — minimalist, no-fluff, opinionated; writes smart-to-smart.
- Patagonia — values-driven, MESSAGE OVER SALES ("not here to make a sale").
- Ahrefs — authority through SPECIFICITY + data, not adjectives.
- Apple — confident restraint; short declaratives; shows, never says "amazing."
- Everlane — radical transparency; trust by showing receipts, never claiming "trustworthy."
- Linear — precision, understated authority.
- Stripe — clarity; deep respect for the reader's intelligence.

LOUD (NOT Ryan Realty): Oatly, Liquid Death, Duolingo, Monzo — irreverent, jokey, disruptive.

## The one transferable principle: SPECIFICITY (= show, don't tell)
Replace every abstract claim with a concrete fact.
- "high quality" -> the spec
- "trustworthy" -> the receipts (24 real Google reviews)
- "great service" -> the specific thing done
Test: if the sentence could sit on any brokerage's site, it's TELLING. If only Ryan Realty
could have written it (names a real number / review / action), it's SHOWING.

## NN/g four dimensions — Ryan Realty's locked profile
(NN/g: tone measurably moves perceived trustworthiness, friendliness, desirability.)
- Formal <-> Casual:            PLAIN (a person, not a corporation; not chummy)
- Funny <-> Serious:            SERIOUS (biggest transaction of their life; dry warmth, no jokes)
- Respectful <-> Irreverent:    RESPECTFUL (assume they're smart; never talk down)
- Enthusiastic <-> Matter-of-fact: MATTER-OF-FACT (data + reviews carry it, not exclamation)

## Practice (real assets)
- "We provide exceptional service and honest guidance."  -> show the 24 five-star reviews.
- "Cutting-edge marketing on every listing."             -> "Drone, 3D tour, and video on every home." / show the media.
- "Bend is a thriving market."                           -> "1,840 homes for sale. Median $740,000. 15 days to pending."

## Why the homepages failed (the through-line)
The banned-words gate polices vocabulary but not OVERT STATEMENTS. Lines like "the standard
every home gets" / "you talk to the broker who closes it" contain no banned word yet are pure
telling. The fix is the specificity test above, applied to every line.

## Part 1: Per-Platform Cheat Sheet

### 1.1 TikTok

**What the algorithm rewards (2026)**

The core shift from 2025: topic authority now outweighs raw posting frequency. The platform scores your last 3-4 videos to establish a niche signal before distributing to cold audiences. Three or four high-quality weekly videos outperform daily low-effort content. TikTok has also repositioned itself as a search engine — 64% of Gen Z users search TikTok like Google, and "Search Value" is now a direct ranking metric.

Key signals in priority order:
1. **Watch-through completion rate** — 70%+ needed for strong distribution (up from 50% in 2024). The algorithm measures "Qualified Views" at the 5-second mark. Videos that fail to reach sufficient qualified views get stuck in limited distribution.
2. **Rewatch rate** — 15-20%+ is considered a strong signal.
3. **Saves and shares** — weighted significantly higher than likes. Shares indicate willingness to distribute; saves indicate intent to return.
4. **Engagement velocity** — speed of early responses in the first minutes matters.
5. **Original audio** — gets a distribution boost over borrowed trending sounds.
6. **Search optimization** — the algorithm reads auto-captions and text overlays to match content to search queries.

**Ideal specs**

- Length: 60-90 seconds sweet spot. Property tours can go to 3 minutes if genuinely engaging. 15-second clips underperform.
- Hook: First 3 seconds must hook; algorithm gates at 5 seconds for qualified views.
- Posting cadence: 3-5 per week minimum for real growth. 6-10 for acceleration.
- Best posting window: 6-9 PM, 8 PM peak globally.
- Hashtags: 3-5 specific, descriptive tags. Posts with hashtags get 5% more views and 10% more interactions than no-hashtag posts. Hashtag traffic grew 114% YoY. Location hashtags are highest-ROI for local markets.
- Captions: Use exact phrases buyers type into search ("what $600K buys in Bend Oregon 2026", "best neighborhoods Bend Oregon families").

**What TikTok punishes**

- Generic content without a clear niche
- Pure trend-chasing with no original contribution
- Watermarked cross-posts from other platforms
- More than 10 reposts in 30 days (excluded from recommendations)

### 1.2 Instagram Reels

**What the algorithm rewards (2026)**

The "Your Algorithm" feature launched December 2025 and went fully global in early 2026 — users can now explicitly declare topics they want more of. This means topic-consistent accounts get stronger distribution. Instagram now also uses fingerprinting to track which account first uploaded a video, and applies an "Aggregator Penalty" to accounts reposting without meaningful original contribution.

Key signals in priority order:
1. **Sends per reach** — private DM shares are the strongest signal, weighted 3-5x higher than likes. A viewer DMing your Reel to a friend is the highest-value action you can earn.
2. **Views (including replays)** — every instance the post appears on a screen counts, including re-watches.
3. **Retention past 3 seconds** — the first threshold. Full-watch retention matters for Explore distribution.
4. **Saves** — weighted roughly 3x higher than likes. Signals lasting value.
5. **Account authority** — consistent engagement history and relationship signals.
6. **Content originality** — original audio, original footage, original creative framing.

Likes are explicitly the weakest signal in Instagram's 2026 hierarchy.

**Ideal specs**

- Length: 15-90 seconds for discovery. Videos up to 3 minutes now eligible for Explore if retention is strong.
- Posting cadence: 1-2 per day optimal. Real growth threshold begins at 3-5 per week. Replying to comments within 60 minutes of posting generates ~21% engagement lift.
- Carousels: Limit doubled to 20 slides. Good for reference-style content (comparison posts, checklists, neighborhood breakdowns). Carousel dwell time is valued.
- Hashtags: 9-15 highly relevant tags. Mix location + niche + broad.
- Audio: Trending audio works for reach in its first 48 hours. Original VO or ambient gets algorithmic boost and avoids the oversaturation window.

**Critical distinction from TikTok**

Instagram Reels warms up your existing sphere — people already considering you will vet your Reels before reaching out. TikTok finds cold audiences who have never heard of you. Use both: TikTok for reach, Reels for conversion.

### 1.3 YouTube Shorts

**What the algorithm rewards (2026)**

YouTube Shorts now prioritizes viewer satisfaction signals over any vanity metric. The key question the algorithm asks: what does the viewer do after watching? Do they subscribe, watch another video, or swipe away?

Key signals:
1. **Watch-through rate** — single most important metric. Shorts completing at high rates get served to wider audiences.
2. **Post-watch behavior** — subscriptions, subsequent video watches, and channel visits all strengthen distribution.
3. **Interactive signals** — comments, shares, and remixes.
4. **Content-to-audience match** — Shorts drive subscribers into your long-form library. The algorithm values this funnel.

**Ideal specs**

- Length: 13 seconds or 60 seconds perform best algorithmically (completion rates). 15-30 seconds achieve highest completion rates. 60 seconds increases monetization potential.
- Hook: First 3 seconds critical. State the topic within the first few seconds.
- Keyword optimization: Clear keywords in title and description. The Shorts algorithm uses text matching aggressively.
- Cadence: Channels with 200+ Shorts see consistent view compounding. Plan for volume.
- Cross-platform: Same video as TikTok/Reels, but remove watermarks. Add keyword-rich title and description (TikTok captions do not carry over).

**YouTube Shorts vs. Long-Form synergy**

Shorts are not a substitute for long-form — they are a discovery layer that drives subscribers into your long-form content. A viewer who finds a 45-second Shorts clip about Bend's Old Mill District and subscribes will later find your 10-minute neighborhood deep-dive. The two compound each other.

### 1.4 Facebook Reels

**What the algorithm rewards (2026)**

Facebook's 2026 algorithm tightened hard on originality. A "User True Interest Survey" now asks viewers to rate Reels 1-5 for interest accuracy. This data overrides behavioral signal noise (mindless scroll vs. genuine interest). Facebook reports doubled Reels views and engagement since H2 2025.

Key signals:
1. **Content originality** — fingerprinting detects first upload. Original content that Facebook can attribute to you directly gets a major boost.
2. **Completion rate** — decisions made in under 3 seconds. A 15-second video people finish beats a 60-second video people abandon.
3. **High-intent engagement** — shares to stories and saves are worth dramatically more than likes. One share is worth roughly 50 generic likes algorithmically.
4. **Account consistency** — algorithm scans last 9-12 posts to define your "brand tag." Two core themes recommended.

**Critical Facebook insight: Groups over Pages**

Facebook Page organic reach in 2026 averages 1-6% of followers. Facebook Groups reach 20-40% of members per post. Groups are the single most powerful free distribution channel on Facebook. For local real estate: a "Bend Oregon Real Estate Q&A" or "Central Oregon First-Time Buyers" group outperforms a page strategy 5-10x on organic reach.

**Ideal specs**

- Length: 15-30 seconds completes better. Original Reels. 
- First 6 hours: algorithm's testing period — interaction density is critical in this window.
- Captions required: most Facebook video is watched muted.
- Posting cadence: 3-5 per week on your Page. Groups can be higher frequency.

### 1.5 LinkedIn

**What the algorithm rewards (2026)**

LinkedIn's 2026 algorithm evaluates professional content through three signals: engagement quality in the first 60 minutes, sustained dwell time, and creator authenticity (demonstrated expertise, not corporate speak). Vertical short-form video (portrait 1080x1920, 15-90 seconds) is currently receiving an extra non-follower distribution boost not seen in any other format.

Key data:
- Videos drive 5x higher interaction rates than text posts.
- LinkedIn Live receives 7x more reactions than standard video uploads.
- Personal stories and contrarian takes outperform polished corporate content.
- Critical engagement window: 1-3 hours after posting. Early "See More" clicks determine growth trajectory.
- Captions are mandatory: LinkedIn video is predominantly watched on mute.

**For Ryan Realty specifically**

LinkedIn is the highest-intent buyer and referral surface for move-up buyers, relocation clients, and professional investors. A 60-second vertical video breaking down Bend's market or cost-of-living attracts professionals actively researching the move — the exact demographic Ryan Realty serves. Content strategy: 2-5 posts per week, personal authentic framing, market data presented conversationally.

### 1.6 YouTube Long-Form

**The case for long-form in real estate**

Long-form YouTube (8-12 minutes, 1920x1080) functions as a search-driven inbound engine, not a discovery platform. Viewers find your content through search, watch 8 minutes, and arrive in your DMs already knowing your face, your market analysis style, and your philosophy. By the time they contact you, the sale is 70% closed.

Case in point: a channel that generated $400K GCI had only 500 subscribers — but those 500 people were exclusively high-intent Bend-area buyers/sellers. Subscriber count is not the metric.

**What converts**

High-intent search topics:
- "Moving to Bend Oregon 2026" 
- "Living in Bend Oregon cost of living"
- "Bend Oregon neighborhoods pros and cons"
- "Bend Oregon real estate market 2026"
- "Sunriver Oregon vs Bend Oregon"
- "Central Oregon investment property"

**Structure that works**

1. Open (within 30 seconds): clearly identify who this video is for and what they will learn
2. Content: specific, actionable neighborhood, pricing, and lifestyle trade-offs
3. Close: direct CTA with contact info, lead magnet, or LP link

**Realistic timeline**

- Months 1-2: Foundation, first traction
- Months 2-4: First inbound leads appear
- Months 6-12: Consistent pipeline, potentially replacing paid lead sources
- Year 2: 3-8 closings per year from YouTube alone

**Length and frequency**

7-12 minutes per video. 1-2 videos per week. Commit for 12 months before evaluating. Every video compresses into a Short for cross-platform distribution.

## Part 2: Winning vs. Dead Formats

### What is winning in 2026

**1. Hyperlocal neighborhood content (rising, high longevity)**  
"Living in [Neighborhood]" videos — specific street-level POV, schools, commute, vibe, price anchor. The format wins because national platforms cannot replicate local knowledge. A single well-optimized neighborhood guide drives 200-500 unique monthly visitors for years. On YouTube, this is evergreen. On TikTok/Reels, it answers the search queries buyers are typing.

**2. "What $X buys you in [City]" (still dominant, evolution required)**  
The format is not dead but it is crowded. What separates winners: the three-property comparison at different price points (e.g., $450K / $650K / $950K in Bend) with honest trade-off framing, not just listing features. The hook reveals something surprising ("$650K doesn't buy what you think in Bend right now").

**3. Market myth-busting (rising)**  
Challenges one specific belief buyers or sellers hold that is factually wrong. Works because it provides relief, signals trustworthiness, and drives saves + shares. The best versions are direct-to-camera with no framing, just the correction. Example: "You don't need 20% down — the average first-time buyer put in 6% in 2026."

**4. Data drop (rising, underused by most agents)**  
One surprising market stat, presented visually with a tight hook. Works because it establishes authority through specific numbers and cannot be easily copied without local data access. Best on TikTok and YouTube Shorts as a 30-45 second standalone.

**5. Day-in-the-life and behind-the-scenes (strong for trust)**  
Closing day, offer night, difficult client conversation (anonymized), the reality of a slow market — authentic moments that humanize the brokerage. These drive DM conversions more than broad-reach formats. Best on Stories and in-feed Reels.

**6. POV storytelling (native format, platform-algorithm-friendly)**  
"POV: you just made an offer above asking in Bend" or "POV: your listing sat 60 days and here's what we did." The POV frame signals authenticity, triggers aspiration or relatability, and gets algorithmically boosted as a native short-form format.

**7. Episodic / series content (compounding organic growth)**  
A weekly "Central Oregon Market Minute" or "Bend hidden gem" series trains the algorithm to predict your topic and trains audiences to expect your next installment. Agents who interview local cafe owners, dog-park volunteers, and restaurateurs and weave that into a community spotlight series grow local following faster than single-topic videos. Series completion rates compound: fans who found episode 4 go back to episodes 1-3.

### What is dead or saturated in 2026

**Generic listing tours** — walking through a house with background music and a voiceover listing features. The format peaked in 2023. No narrative, no contrast, no hook. Viewers scroll past because they have seen 10,000 of these and learned nothing.

**"Just listed" static posts** — a photo of a house with price, beds, baths, and a link. No reach. No distribution. These belong in Stories, not in-feed.

**Broad motivational content** — "It's a great time to buy!" or "The market is shifting!" with no specific data. Punished by every algorithm as generic. Drives no saves, no shares, no DMs.

**Talking head without a specific hook** — an agent facing the camera saying "Hey everyone, I'm here to talk about the Bend market today." Zero hook, zero curiosity gap, immediate scroll.

**High-volume low-quality posting** — the Metricool 2026 study analyzed 2.3M+ posts and found that 72% growth in video posts and 140% growth in carousels still produced dropping performance metrics across the board. More is not better. Quality and relevance are the separating factors.

**Carousel as primary format on TikTok** — carousels generate 5x fewer views and 6x fewer interactions than video on TikTok (Metricool 2026 data). Carousels belong on Instagram and LinkedIn, not TikTok.

## Part 3: Local and Area Lifestyle Content

### Why local content outperforms listing content for reach

Local lifestyle content reaches people before they are actively searching for an agent. Someone watching "Top 7 restaurants in Bend Oregon" is at the awareness stage — they may be six months from looking at homes, but they associate the local knowledge and warmth of your account with Ryan Realty. When they are ready, you are already top of mind.

The Bend Epicurean case: launched September 2023, became Central Oregon's fastest-growing social media channel by averaging 50+ organic followers daily through authentic community-focused content. This was zero-budget, zero-paid-distribution growth through local lifestyle video.

### Format patterns for local content

**Hidden gem format**  
"Stop scrolling if you live in Bend — this spot exists and most people drive past it every day." Short (30-45 seconds), specific (named location, specific visual), no filler. Gets shares from locals who feel seen and saves from visitors who bookmark it.

**Seasonal anchor**  
Bend has a naturally calendar-driven story: ski season, river float season, fall color season, wildfire smoke reality, the actual smell of ponderosa pine. Seasonal content ranks in local search when people plan visits and relocations.

**Community spotlight**  
Interview the owner of Thump Coffee or the organizer of a farmers market. Position Ryan Realty as the entity that tells Bend's stories, not the entity that sells houses. The conversion is long-term brand equity, not direct lead generation.

**Events-based content**  
Bend has genuine event inventory: Pole Pedal Paddle, Bachelorette capital energy, the Old Mill District calendar. Event-preview videos that go up 5-7 days before the event rank in search during the lead-up window.

**Local SEO integration**  
Use location tags on every video (city, neighborhood, venue). TikTok's Local Feed feature now surfaces content to users based on their physical location. A video tagged to Bend's Westside gets served to people physically in the Westside. This is high-intent local distribution at zero cost.

### Hashtag strategy for local 2026

- Instagram: 9-15 tags. Mix: 3 broad (#bendoregon, #centraloregon, #realestate), 5 niche (#bendlifestyle, #bendhomes, #oregonrealtor), 3-4 location-specific (#westsidbend, #oldmilldistrict, #discoverywestbend).
- TikTok: 3-5 tags. Laser-relevant. #bendoregon, #centraloregonrealestate, #bendlifestyle. Local hashtag traffic is highest-ROI.
- YouTube: Keywords in title and description. Tags secondary. "Bend Oregon" + specific neighborhood or topic in the title is the primary search surface.
- Hashtags as SEO: Use exact phrases your buyer types, not aspirational marketing language.

## Part 4: AI-Native Viral Formats

### Formats that only exist because of AI

**Earth zoom / location reveal**  
Start at a backyard, pool, or property entrance. Zoom out through neighborhood, city, mountain range, into space — then zoom back down to the property from orbit. Works because it is visually impossible with physical cameras, creates a "how did they make that" reaction, and embeds geographic context in an unmistakably modern way. One creator's most viral Reel (8.2M views) used this format for a single location reveal. Tools: Higgsfield AI, Revid.ai, media.io earth-zoom generator.

**Impossible transitions / style morphs**  
A property morphs from its current state to a renovated version, or from one architectural style to another. A 90-second morphing restaurant video received 247M views across platforms in 2026 (source: is4.ai). For real estate: before/after transformations that are impossible to shoot physically — vacant lot to finished home, or winter to summer of the same property exterior.

**AI-generated b-roll for non-visual properties**  
A vacant lot, a commercial property, or a listing with poor photography can be visualized at its future potential using AI-generated footage as supplementary b-roll. Disclosed as rendered visualization. Prevents the dead-listing problem of "no photos, no Reels."

**AI avatar explainers**  
An AI presenter explains market data, neighborhood context, or buyer tips in a brand-consistent format. Works when the content is information-dense (market reports, buying process education) and the human alternative is a static infographic. The risk: uncanny valley kills trust. Use only when the avatar is clearly branded, the content is clearly explained, and the format signals AI without pretending to be human.

**Cinematic transitions between listing photos**  
Ken Burns-style parallax motion applied to listing photos, with smooth depth-based motion that creates the impression of camera movement inside a static image. Currently the core of Ryan Realty's Remotion-based video pipeline. This is AI-assisted, not pure AI generation, and is increasingly the baseline expectation for property video.

### The slop line: what distinguishes cinematic AI from cheap AI

**Signs of cheap AI video (avoid)**

- **Extra or fused fingers** — hands are the most consistent tell of generative video.
- **Character inconsistency across cuts** — skin tone, hair shape, and facial proportions shift between clips. Viewers feel this subconsciously even without naming it.
- **Waxy, pore-free skin** — over-smoothing from diffusion model denoising. Looks like mannequin skin.
- **Object impermanence** — a mug migrates, a logo reshapes, furniture rearranges between shots. AI models do not understand object permanence.
- **Temporal instability / shimmering** — background brightness pulses, textures crawl. Frame-to-frame inconsistency in lighting computation.
- **Weightless camera movement** — camera floats without emotional purpose or physical logic. No resistance, no inertia.
- **Hollow voice sync** — AI voiceover with a reading-from-script quality triggers uncanny valley even in viewers who cannot name what is wrong.
- **Overly complex prompts** — counterintuitively, loading the prompt with layered directives produces worse results. Fewer, focused instructions.

**What cinematic AI looks like**

- Temporal stability: backgrounds do not shimmer, textures do not crawl.
- Intentional camera motion with emotional purpose — push-in on a reveal, slow pan across a view, not random float.
- Prompt discipline: one scene, one instruction, one visual outcome.
- Audio that is human-voiced (ElevenLabs Victoria, properly prompted) or licensed music — never synthetic robotic TTS.
- Clear disclosure when appropriate: AI-rendered visualization is credible; fake-human AI is not.

**Best tools by function (2026)**

- Natural human movement + lip-sync: Kling AI
- Cinematic language / scene composition: Google Veo 3
- Character consistency across long-form videos: Intellemo AI
- Earth zoom effect: Higgsfield AI, Revid.ai
- Upscaling without soap-opera smoothing: Topaz Starlight 2.5
- Composited photo-to-video motion (Ryan Realty's current stack): Remotion + ElevenLabs + static photo KenBurns

## Part 5: Hook Library — 30 Copy-Ready Patterns

### Psychological framework

Six mechanisms drive short-form hook performance:
1. **Curiosity gap** — information void the viewer must close
2. **Problem amplification** — names a frustration they feel but haven't articulated
3. **Authority challenge** — questions conventional wisdom
4. **Transformation promise** — shows aspirational end result
5. **Relatability** — makes viewer feel understood
6. **Pattern interrupt** — visual or verbal surprise that breaks the scroll

All strong hooks activate at least one. The best activate two or three.

### Real estate + local hooks: copy-ready

**Curiosity gap / secret reveal**
1. "This $750K Bend home has something the listing photos don't show you."
2. "Nobody's talking about what happened to Westside Bend prices in the last 90 days."
3. "The neighborhood in Central Oregon that's about to blow up — and why most people haven't heard of it yet."
4. "I've been a principal broker for 12 years. Here's the advice I give my friends that I'd never put in a listing description."
5. "There's a spot in Bend that locals go out of their way to protect from becoming Instagram-famous."

**Problem amplification**
6. "If you're searching for homes in Bend right now and wondering why nothing makes sense — this is why."
7. "You searched for a 3-bed in Bend under $600K. Here's the honest picture of what that actually gets you."
8. "Every buyer I talk to makes the same mistake before their first offer. Here's what it costs them."
9. "Sellers in Bend are pricing wrong right now and it's sitting on the market. Here's the pattern."

**Authority challenge / myth-busting**
10. "Real estate myth: you need 20% down to buy in Bend. The average first-time buyer put in 6% in 2026."
11. "Stop doing open houses. Here's what actually sells a home in Central Oregon right now."
12. "The Bend market is not 'crashing' and it is not 'on fire.' Here's the actual number."
13. "Most agents will tell you to repaint before listing. Here's when that's wrong."
14. "I'm going to say something that might make other agents in Bend uncomfortable."

**POV**
15. "POV: you just moved to Bend from Portland and you're at the Saturday farmers market for the first time."
16. "POV: you're 38 years old, you got priced out of the Bay Area, and Bend just made sense."
17. "POV: your Bend listing just got three offers in 4 days. Here's what we did differently."
18. "POV: you finally have the land you always wanted in Central Oregon. What it actually cost."

**Transformation / before-after**
19. "This Bend home sat on the market for 60 days. We made four changes. It sold in 9."
20. "Before this home hit the market vs. the night of the open house — same house, completely different result."
21. "What $750K bought you in Bend in 2021 vs. what it buys right now. The shift is real."

**Data drop**
22. "Bend's median home price just crossed a threshold we haven't seen since 2022. Here's what that means for you."
23. "38 days on market. That's the current average in Central Oregon. Two years ago it was 11."
24. "One in four Bend buyers in 2026 came from California. Here's how that's changing the market."
25. "Bend home inventory is at X months of supply. That puts us in [seller/balanced/buyer] market territory."

**Local lifestyle**
26. "Stop scrolling if you live in Bend — this trail exists 12 minutes from downtown and most people drive past the trailhead."
27. "Things to do in Bend this weekend that aren't [obvious tourist trap]."
28. "The Bend neighborhood nobody posts about. And why it might be the best value left in Central Oregon."

## Part 6: Audio Strategy

### 2026 positioning: original audio has the algorithmic edge

TikTok rebalanced in 2025 to reward original audio more heavily. Original audio receives an explicit distribution boost. This is a meaningful shift from 2023-2024 when trending sounds were the primary reach lever.

### Trending audio: when and how to use it

Trending sounds still work, but only if:
- You use the sound within its first 48 hours of trending (after day 7-10, the sound is oversaturated and the algorithm deprioritizes late entries).
- Your content provides a genuine hook layered on top of the sound — the algorithm must be able to associate your video with the trending audio. A conflicting VO on top of a trending music track splits the signal.
- The sound fits your content authentically, not as a bolt-on.

Trending audio provides the highest viral potential in its early window. Original audio provides compounding authority and discoverability through search.

### Original VO strategy (Ryan Realty's primary mode)

Victoria (ElevenLabs) is a strategic asset, not just a convenience. A branded voice that appears consistently across all Ryan Realty video trains the audience to recognize the sound before the visual. The voice becomes a brand signal.

Key rules:
- Keep sentences short, two clauses maximum. Natural speech rhythm.
- Spell numbers for TTS input: "four hundred seventy-five thousand" not "475,000."
- IPA phonemes for Deschutes, Tumalo, Paulina — local pronunciation signals authentic local knowledge.
- Chain `previous_text` across lines for prosody continuity.
- The voice should feel like a trusted local who knows the market — not a narrator.

### Music strategy

- Background music: beat-synced ambient, not trending pop. The music frames mood without competing with VO.
- For non-VO content (property walk, time-lapse, B-roll montage): trending instrumental or licensed ambient. Always rights-cleared (no TikTok watermark equals no problem on YouTube/Reels).
- Avoid copyrighted music in YouTube Shorts — copyright strikes suppress distribution and remove monetization eligibility.
- Beat-sync visual cuts to music increases retention measurably. Cuts that land on a beat feel intentional; cuts between beats feel sloppy.

## Part 7: Organic Growth Mechanics

### The compound model

Organic growth on short-form is not linear — it compounds. The mechanisms:

**Consistency creates algorithmic trust.** Posting in 20+ consecutive weeks produces roughly 450% more engagement per post than sporadic posting (Buffer 2026 creator data). The algorithm learns your topic signal and begins pre-distributing to matched audiences.

**Replies compound reach.** Replying to comments on Instagram within 60 minutes generates 21% higher engagement on subsequent posts. On LinkedIn, the lift is 30%. On Threads, 42%. Comment replies are the cheapest reach-amplifier available.

**Series format creates return audience.** A viewer who discovered episode 4 of "Central Oregon Market Minute" goes back to episodes 1-3. That watch history trains the algorithm to serve your channel to similar users. Series format is organic compounding at the account level.

**YouTube compounds differently.** YouTube content is searchable forever. A neighborhood guide published today ranks in search results for years. One well-optimized "Moving to Bend Oregon" video generates leads for 36 months. This is fundamentally different from Reels/TikTok, which have a 24-48 hour peak distribution window.

### Cross-posting: the right way

Same video can go to TikTok, IG Reels, YouTube Shorts, and Facebook Reels in the same hour — with modifications:

1. **Film in 9:16 native.** Shoot portrait, never crop from landscape.
2. **Remove watermarks.** Watermarked cross-posts receive 30-50% less distribution. TikTok-watermarked videos are explicitly penalized by Instagram and YouTube.
3. **Customize captions per platform.** Identical captions can trigger duplicate content detection. Adjust hashtags and keyword framing per platform norms.
4. **Customize audio.** Trending audio on TikTok does not confer the same boost on YouTube Shorts.
5. **Write a keyword-rich title for YouTube** — YouTube Shorts discovery is search-driven, not caption-driven.
6. **Never repost more than 10 times in 30 days** — TikTok excludes accounts from recommendations at that threshold.

### Profile optimization

- Bio: one-line value proposition + single link. "Bend's market data, honest opinions, and hyperlocal expertise." Not "Principal Broker, licensed 20XX, serving Bend."
- Profile photo: broker headshot (transparent PNG from design system), not logo.
- Link in bio: rotate to highest-converting CTA (free home value report, neighborhood guide download, calendar link).
- Pinned posts: 3 pinned posts on TikTok and Instagram. Pin your best-performing hook video, your most-shared market data video, and your clearest CTA video.

### What compounds vs. what is a one-time spike

**Compounds:**
- YouTube long-form neighborhood guides (search traffic, evergreen)
- Series formats with recurring viewership
- Comment engagement (algorithmic trust signal)
- Profile-level topic consistency (algorithm learns your niche)
- Local hashtag authority (location-tag association over time)

**One-time spike:**
- Trending audio (decays in days)
- Trending topic newsjack without evergreen value
- Viral meme formats (high reach, low conversion, no replay)
- Promoted/boosted posts without organic follow-through

## Part 9: Application Notes for Ryan Realty

### Three highest-leverage format bets right now

**Bet 1: Hyperlocal neighborhood video series on YouTube + Shorts (highest ROI, longest shelf life)**  
A weekly "Living in [Neighborhood] — Bend Oregon" series on YouTube (8-10 min each) with the same video cut to a 45-second Short. Targets the highest-intent buyer: someone actively researching a relocation to Bend. YouTube content compounds — a Westside Bend neighborhood guide published this week ranks in search for 2-3 years. Build 12 episodes covering every major Bend neighborhood + the resort communities. Victoria VO, Ryan Realty b-roll, authentic lifestyle footage.

**Bet 2: Monthly market data drop — data-led, one surprising number (highest shares on TikTok/Reels)**  
Not a full market report recap. One number, presented with contrast and a genuine hook. "Bend's median list price just crossed $749K for the first time since [date]. Here's the three things driving it and what it means if you're thinking about selling this fall." 45-60 seconds. Victoria VO, single stat on screen, clean. Data from Supabase, verified per protocol. This format drives saves and shares — the highest-value signals for both TikTok and Instagram.

**Bet 3: "What $X buys in Bend" — three-tier version (proven format, local search gold)**  
Three price points ($450K / $650K / $850K), three real properties, honest trade-off framing. Run on TikTok and Reels for discovery. Title-optimized for YouTube Shorts. The hook reveals something buyers do not expect ("$650K doesn't get you a garage in the Westside right now — but here's what it does get you"). This is the format that converts viewers into DMs fastest, because it answers the exact question buyers are Googling and now TikToking.

### Current hook and hold rules

- **Hook timing:** On-screen text and motion by frame 12 (0.4 seconds). First spoken word is content, not greeting. Specific element in the hook: a number, a place name, a contradiction, or a visual surprise.
- **Hold mechanics:** One new piece of information every 4-6 seconds. No beat over 4 seconds. Pattern interrupt at 25% and 50% of video duration.
- **Completion target:** 70%+ on TikTok. 3-second mark on Instagram Reels. 5-second "qualified view" on TikTok is the algorithmic gate.
- **Saves and sends** are the engagement actions worth optimizing toward — not likes, not comments.

### Stop doing this, start doing that

**Stop:** Generic listing tour format — walk through the property, name the features, add background music, post. This format is algorithmically invisible and conversion-dead in 2026.

**Start:** Narrative market content where each video answers one specific question a real buyer or seller is asking right now. Every video should be answerable to the question "why would someone save or share this?" If the answer is "they wouldn't," the concept needs to change before production begins. The video is not an advertisement for a listing — it is a piece of information that happens to come from Ryan Realty.

The underlying shift: **Ryan Realty is building a media operation that happens to sell real estate, not a real estate operation that happens to post content.**

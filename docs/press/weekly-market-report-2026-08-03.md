## Methodology

Months of supply = active listings divided by average monthly closings over the last 6 months (homes closed in the last 6 months divided by 6). Under 4 months is a seller's market, 4 to 6 is balanced, over 6 is a buyer's market.

Active-inventory, median-list-price, and months-of-supply figures come from `market_pulse_live` (refreshed every 10 to 15 minutes). Median closed price, median days on market, and the 90-day closed-sale count come from `market_stats_cache`, rolling 90-day window (refreshed every 6 hours). Both are computed server-side from the full listings feed, not aggregated ad hoc.

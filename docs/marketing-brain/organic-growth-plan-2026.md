## 0. The honest answer to "10K followers on every channel"

**It is not realistic to hit 10,000 followers on every channel in 12 months. It IS realistic to hit 10,000 followers on the right 4–5 channels in 12–24 months, and to use cross-syndication so the other channels grow as a free byproduct.**

Three things you should hear before reading further:

1. **The path is dictated by channel math, not effort.** TikTok's interest-graph algorithm gives a 0-follower account the same initial distribution as a 1M-follower account ([Adpicto 2026](https://www.adpicto.com/en/blog/tiktok-algorithm-2026-business-guide); [TikTok official](https://newsroom.tiktok.com/introducing-tiktok-next-2026-our-trend-forecast-for-marketers-for-the-year-ahead)). Facebook's algorithm caps a Page post at **1.65% of follower reach** ([YourTabula 2026](https://yourtabula.com/blog/organic-social-media-2026/)). These are structural ceilings — not effort problems. They dictate which channel can compound to 10K in 9 months vs. 24+ months.
2. **The unifying lever is hyper-local — the Bend/Tumalo niche.** Every winning real estate creator we studied owned a specific geography ([Kina DeSantis, Irvine](https://www.housingwire.com/articles/tiktok-real-estate/); [Glennda Baker, Atlanta](https://www.creatorspotlight.com/p/glennda-baker); [Brad White, NE Florida](https://www.cp-mtg.com/rock-and-roll-realtor-brad-white-10m-100k-followers/); [Hunter Ka'imi, Bellingham WA — 1.4M followers from a city of 90K](https://www.cascadiadaily.com/2026/mar/18/how-bellinghams-best-known-influencer-uses-his-reach-for-good/)). The Bend TikTok / YouTube real estate niche is **essentially unoccupied** (verified in existing competitor recon). Niche-down is the moat.
3. **You already have 90% of the automation infrastructure.** 60+ producers, a brain that generates briefs weekly, a publisher cron that posts every 10 minutes, daily snapshots of every channel, daily competitor recon. The "hands-off" part is mostly **plumbing the growth-decision logic into the brain you already built** — not building a new stack.

## 1. The doctrine (the strategy in one paragraph)

> **Become locally famous in Bend before you try to be globally interesting.** TikTok first (algorithm rewards new accounts in unoccupied niches). Daily posting, batched once a month, in a "Kina DeSantis" mode: one niche (move-up buyer or relocation buyer), one CTA repeated on every video, education over entertainment. Cross-channel repurpose every Tier-1 piece automatically. Build a Facebook Group as the lead engine separately from the FB Page. Use LinkedIn for the relocating tech-worker (this is Bend's highest-ticket buyer — they live on LinkedIn). YouTube long-form for compounding search traffic. Matt's face on camera; brokerage logo only at the end card. Restraint is the luxury signal.

This doctrine is anchored to four pieces of evidence:

- **Kina DeSantis built a $102M brokerage with 27,400 TikTok followers** by answering one buyer question per video, daily, hyper-local to Irvine — never chasing virality ([HousingWire 2025](https://www.housingwire.com/articles/tiktok-real-estate/); [Knwn Local interview April 2026](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=VbvlbR45bQk)). Closest comparable analog to Ryan Realty.
- **Glennda Baker books 30% of her business from one 4-hour batch shoot per month** (30–50 videos produced in one session, dripped daily) ([Creator Spotlight 2024](https://www.creatorspotlight.com/p/glennda-baker)). This is the production system that makes hands-off possible.
- **Laura Griffin generated $22M GCI from a single Facebook Group** ("The Loudoun Moms"), spending $0 on lead gen, by treating the Group like a 90/10 community-first conversation ([HousingWire 2025/26](https://www.housingwire.com/articles/get-leads-from-real-estate-facebook-groups/)). Loudoun County and Bend are structurally similar (suburban/exurban, educated move-up demographic, relocation-driven).
- **Jon Burton in Durango, CO (population 20,000 — smaller than Bend) generated $400K+ GCI from YouTube alone** with hyperlocal seller-focused content ([Passive Prospecting 2026](https://www.passiveprospecting.com/how-to-generate-seller-leads-w-youtube-for-real-estate-2026)). Proves small-market YouTube works.

The strategy is not invented. It is the documented pattern across every winning case study.

## 5. Cross-channel repurposing engine (the single highest-leverage automation)

Every Tier-1 asset built by an existing producer should automatically fan out to 6+ channel-specific variants. **This is the #1 lever for hitting 10K on multiple channels without multiplying production effort.**

### Producer to author: `cross-channel-repurpose`

**Inputs:**
- Source asset path (MP4, image, PDF, or text)
- Source format (long-form-video, carousel, single-post, etc.)

**Outputs:**

**Approval gate:**

**Estimated build:** 2–3 days. Highest-ROI single producer in the entire registry once built.

## 6. Approval gate tiering (the single biggest hands-off bottleneck)

Today, Matt approves every piece. This is the bottleneck. The fix is a 3-tier gate.

| Tier | Content type | Approval mode | Justification |
|---|---|---|---|
| **T1 — Matt explicit** | Net-new market report, net-new listing tour, news-clip, anything with on-camera Matt, any net-new long-form video | matt-review-draft | Data accuracy mandate + brand on the line |
| **T2 — Brand-voice + scorecard auto-approve** | Cross-channel repurpose of T1-approved content, daily market_pulse_short, templated `sold-deal-summary`, `under-contract-announcement`, GBP post, Pinterest pin, X thread, Threads post | auto if brand-voice passes + scorecard ≥ format floor + data trace present | Already approved upstream; mechanical reformatting; templated patterns |

The `publisher-sweep` cron already handles `status='approved'` → publish; this change auto-approves T2 rows after `producer-runtime` completes the voice + scorecard checks.

**Expected impact:** Matt's daily approval load drops from ~10 pieces/day to ~2/day (the T1 originals). The T2 repurposes flow through unsupervised. **This single change unlocks "hands-off" growth.**

## 7. Engagement automation — the dark matter of growth

Three automations:

### 7.1 ManyChat keyword loop (IG only — set up today)

- Setup: 2 hours. Cost: $15/mo Pro plan.
- Keywords: `BEND`, `MOVE`, `SOLD`, `TUMALO`, `RELOCATE`
- Each triggers an auto-DM with a resource + a qualifying question + a "reply STOP to opt out"

### 7.2 Golden-hour reply bot (extends existing `marketing-inbox-poll`)

- Surface in `/admin/inbox` queue with a "reply now" button
- Matt (or VA) responds within 60 min — captures the +9% FB / +21% IG / +30% LinkedIn / +42% Threads engagement lift per [Buffer](https://buffer.com/resources/state-of-social-media-engagement-2026/)

### 7.3 IG Broadcast Channel + LinkedIn Newsletter (one-time setup + weekly cadence)

- Both ship today's content via inbox delivery, bypassing the feed algorithm entirely
- Broadcast Channel: 1.5B messages/mo platform-wide; opt-in subscribers convert to follows
- LinkedIn Newsletter: 28M LinkedIn members subscribe to at least one newsletter; launch notification reaches all current followers once

## 11. Honest risks and scenarios

### What could go wrong

1. **No viral hit in 6 months.** Realistic. Most accounts never have one. The plan still works — Kina DeSantis built $102M with **zero viral hits and 27,400 followers**. The trajectory is slower (closer to 18-month case) but still ends at 10K on the priority channels.
2. **Matt can't sustain the 4hr/mo batch shoot.** This is the biggest single failure mode. If Matt skips the batch shoot for 2 consecutive months, the producer queue runs dry on Matt-on-camera content. The brain falls back to data-only producers (`market_pulse_short`, `market-data-video`, `data_viz_video`) which are good but don't carry the same personal-brand signal. **Mitigation:** scheduled monthly calendar block, treated as immovable.
3. **TikTok production OAuth blocked.** Sandbox token has been expiring. If TikTok dev approval takes weeks, the plan's P0 channel is hobbled. **Mitigation:** start the OAuth flow this week; fall back to manual posting via TikTok app for first month if needed (still publishes content to the audience while OAuth resolves).
4. **FB Group fails to cross 500-member threshold.** Without 500 members, Facebook doesn't promote it organically. **Mitigation:** the $30–50 boost budget is allocated specifically for this; if needed, expand to $100. Below the threshold for 6+ months would be the signal to either invest more in boost or accept FB as a P3 channel.
5. **LinkedIn newsletter access denied.** Some accounts wait 4+ months for Creator-mode + newsletter access. **Mitigation:** the plan's W1–8 doesn't depend on newsletter — it depends on 3–5x/wk posting. Newsletter is a month-9 unlock; if delayed to month 12, no plan damage.
6. **Approval-gate auto-approval ships a bad piece.** A T2 auto-approval that passes brand-voice + scorecard but turns out to be tone-deaf in market context. **Mitigation:** the auto-approval logic includes a 15-min hold window where Matt can override; daily digest surfaces yesterday's T2 publishes for retroactive flagging.

### Scenarios — what 18 months actually looks like

| Scenario | Probability | Result at month 18 |
|---|---|---|
| **Best case** (consistent execution + 1 viral hit per channel) | 15% | 5 channels at 10K+; TikTok possibly 25K+; FB Group 10K+ members |
| **Base case** (consistent execution, no viral hits) | 60% | 3 channels at 10K (TikTok, IG, FB Group-or-Page combined); LinkedIn ~6–8K; YouTube ~3K subs but generating consistent leads |
| **Slow case** (Matt misses 2+ batch shoots; OAuth delays; competition catches on to Bend niche) | 20% | 1–2 channels at 10K; others at 3–6K; lead flow still grows but slower than projection |
| **Fail case** (Matt stops engaging with the system within 90 days) | 5% | Plan reverts to current state (manual, ad-hoc) |

The plan's success is **most sensitive to consistency**, not to budget, skill, or virality. Buffer's 100K-user study: creators posting 20+ of 26 weeks get **450% more engagement** than sporadic posters. The automated publishing pipeline solves the sporadic problem if Matt keeps feeding it (batch shoots).

# CMA letter — 15991 Falcon, La Pine
Date: 2026-09-06
Reviewed from: Review letter /view screenshot (full scroll)
Who: seller (or seller prospect) reading a valuation letter on phone or desktop
Job: trust the number, see why, know what to do next — not read a research dump

## Verdict
It looks like a spreadsheet with a photo on top. The $497,800/900 is the only clear beat; everything after is same-weight cards, repeated photos, tiny charts, and comps told three times. Cut hard. Make it a letter, not a dashboard printout.

## Fix first
1. **Length / repetition (blocker).** Hero photo repeats full-bleed after the description. Comps appear as a grid, a wide table, a pin map, then full-width cards for each. Cost: seller never reaches “What’s next.” Fix: one hero photo; comps once (3–5 best with photo + one-line why); table as optional “See all comps” expand or print appendix — not all four views inline.
2. **Flat hierarchy (blocker).** Every section is the same white card on gray. Headers look alike. Cost: no sense of what matters after the price. Fix: three acts only — (1) the number + one paragraph why, (2) comps that prove it, (3) next step / call Matt. Demote charts and raw MLS fields below a fold or into “Details.”
3. **Charts unreadable on the letter (blocker on mobile).** Sparklines and multi-year lines lack readable labels; axes tiny. Cost: charts decorate, don’t persuade. Fix: keep at most two charts with large labels (e.g. subject vs comp range; one market trend). Drop the rest or move to a linked market page.
4. **Comparison table on 375 (blocker).** Wide multi-comp table will force horizontal scroll or microscopic type. Fix: stack subject + one comp per block on mobile; never a 8+ column table in the letter view.

## Worth fixing
- **Hero value band.** Green gradient over photo muddies the price box — solid navy/cream band under the photo, or price off the photo entirely.
- **Type.** One sans everywhere reads like admin UI. Use display for the value and section titles; keep tables utilitarian. Match public Ryan Realty serif/sans pairing if this is client-facing.
- **Copy.** “Estimated Value” + date is fine; the wall of property fields (heating, flooring, roof) belongs in Details, not the opening. “What’s Next / Selling Process / Marketing” as three essay blocks — cut to one short next step + Call / Email.
- **Comp cards.** If keeping individuals, cap at 3–4 with a one-line adjuster (“smaller lot,” “updated kitchen”) — not photo + same stats again.
- **Two maps.** Circular location map + comps pin map — keep one (comps map with subject highlighted).
- **Agent close.** Call / Email Matt is clear — keep; don’t bury it under essays.

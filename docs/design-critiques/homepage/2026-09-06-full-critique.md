# Homepage — full page — Ryan Realty (ryan-realty.com)
Date: 2026-09-06
Reviewed from: live URL full scroll (18 shots, hero through footer)
Who: buyers (and sellers / investors) landing cold on Central Oregon inventory
Job: start a search, or get oriented enough to pick a town / call a broker
Owner note: Matt does not like the Atlas (map block)

## Verdict
Below the hero, the page is a long stack of good sections that each ask for attention. The Atlas is the costliest early beat — heavy, color-coded, and in the way of clearer paths (search, featured homes, buy/sell/invest). Cut or defer it; the rest of the page mostly works.

## Path (what the page is today)
Hero search → Atlas map → Buy / Sell / Invest → property-type chips → featured listings → town comparison → market stats + chart → value-my-home → communities → Google reviews → brokers → footer.

## Fix first
1. **Atlas on the homepage (owner dislike + UX cost).** Huge interactive map with heatmap, filters, neighborhood chips, and live counts sits immediately under the hero. Cost: first-time buyers must learn a map UI before they see homes or a simple path. Fix: remove Atlas from the homepage (or collapse to a single “Explore on the map” link into a dedicated map page). Lead with search → featured homes → Buy/Sell/Invest.
2. **Header still fights the primary action.** Full nav + mic + phone + Saved + Menu on every scroll. Same fix as before: simplify desktop chrome.
3. **Homepage tries to do every job at once.** After Atlas: three CTAs, type filters again, listings grid, towns, market essay, seller form, communities, reviews, team. Cost: scroll fatigue; no clear “you’re done / next step.” Fix: pick one primary job above the fold (search or browse homes), one secondary (sell or market), park the rest one click away.

## Worth fixing
- **Property types appear twice** — Atlas sidebar checkboxes and a second chip row under Buy/Sell/Invest. Keep one.
- **Buy / Sell / Invest trio is the clearer post-hero module than Atlas** — keep it; make it the first section after hero/search.
- **Market chart years are color-only** (2023–2026 line colors). Add patterns or direct labels on the lines.
- **Reviews: featured pull quote repeats the first list snippet.** Show three distinct newest reviews beside the pull quote, or drop the duplicate row.
- **Broker cards: photos look tightly cropped** (shoulders/chin framing). Use consistent head-and-shoulders crops with faces fully in frame.
- **Hero search still placeholder-only** (“City, community, or address.”). Persistent label. (Sell form’s “Home address” label is the better pattern — match it.)
- **Mic icon still unlabeled.**

## Minor
- “See all 1,595 single-family homes” outline button is clear — good.
- Footer SEO town links sit in sparse white space; tighten grouping under a “Markets” heading.
- Sticky nav on a page this long makes density worse — another reason to shorten the page.

## Working well
- Hero search CTA is obvious.
- Featured listing photography and price overlays read fast.
- Town comparison (“Where the single-family homes are, and what they cost”) answers a real question with median + active bars.
- Market block answers “buyer’s or seller’s?” in one line, then backs it with months of supply.
- Sell CTA: labeled field + “Value my home” names the outcome.
- Communities list with active counts and “Every community” is scannable.
- Reviews: 5.0 / 25 with full newest quotes builds trust.
- Footer legal + Equal Housing + data source are present.

## Could not judge
- Exact contrast ratios; keyboard/focus on Atlas and chart; mobile layout; empty/error states on search and valuation.
